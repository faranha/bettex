---
name: Simulated staking eligibility
description: Why monthly distribution previews must account for the time tokens were actually eligible.
---

Monthly distribution previews must distinguish a current-balance estimate from an attributed credit for a completed period. Use time-weighted eligible participation for credits, even in fictional periods.

**Why:** A snapshot at the moment a visitor clicks “close” overcredits a last-minute deposit and erases participation already earned before a withdrawal. A chart filter is not a distribution event; only a deliberate period close may add an attributed credit.

**How to apply:** When extending the preview, preserve stake-change timing and cumulative credit/withdrawal totals through local-history limits and migrations. Label a full-month estimate as hypothetical, and keep previously credited balances distinct from future eligibility.
