---
name: Social sharing editorial policy
description: Keep sharing cards current without implying a launch or retaining stale cached artwork.
---
Prefer evergreen community, technology, rules and visual-demo copy on social cards. Include launch dates only when needed and label forecasts explicitly.

**Why:** Social artwork can remain cached and forwarded after page copy changes. Phase labels and timelines can incorrectly suggest an operational release.

**How to apply:** Compare every language's card against current approved product rules, including small badges and mock-window labels. Use a new image URL when replacing an old message; retain legacy images for existing forwarded links.
