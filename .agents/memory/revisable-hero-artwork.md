name: Revisable hero artwork
description: Avoid disconnected compositions when revising market symbols over generated photography.
---

Use one coherent graphic layer for all labels, symbols and connections in revisable hero artwork. Keep the generated photographic background free of baked-in diagram elements.

**Why:** Adding new symbols beside existing rasterized symbols and connections produced a visibly inconsistent composition that the creator rejected as disconnected. Adjusting individual labels did not address the underlying mismatch.

**How to apply:** When the represented methods or markets change, rebuild the whole diagram consistently rather than appending isolated icons to the old raster. Preserve approved copy and surrounding sections. Do not confuse connections between the agent and its markets with a financial-transfer diagram.
