---
name: BETTEX checkout eligibility
description: Payment-provider suitability for BETTEX investment tokens and investor access.
---

Do not treat BETTEX investment tokens as an ordinary digital-goods checkout without confirming that the payment processor approves the specific product and jurisdiction.

**Why:** BETTEX is presented as a token-based investment connected to trading agents. Whop's prohibited-products policy excludes sales of digital assets and offers marketed as lifetime access; Stripe restricts categories of financial and investment services. Connecting a generic checkout without approval could cause failed payments or account restrictions.

**How to apply:** Before enabling real purchases, establish the provider approved for this offering, how tokens are issued or recorded, and how ownership grants investor access. Keep any local simulator clearly separate from actual payments and holdings.
