name: BETTEX content source precedence
description: How to reconcile legacy whitepaper wording with creator-approved product rules.
---

When revising BETTEX's presentation, use the creator's latest explicit instructions and the approved product rules in replit.md as the source of truth. Do not restore a rule merely because it appears in the Git baseline of the whitepaper.

**Why:** The whitepaper previously retained older issuance, pricing and payout-calendar rules after the rest of the presentation had adopted newer approved rules. Preserving that baseline during a terminology revision would preserve contradictory financial information.

**How to apply:** Audit substantive figures and rules across the landing, app presentation and whitepaper together. Distinguish alignment with an already approved rule from introducing a new economic rule; do not invent allocations, vesting dates or guarantees.
