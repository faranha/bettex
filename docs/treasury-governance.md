Divergence between journal and blockchain blocks new movements. ## Roles, permission and segregation Specific permissions in `accounts`: `treasury_view`, `treasury_propose`, `treasury_approve`, `treasury_execute`, `treasury_pause`, `treasury_policy`, `treasury_activate`. Existing `treasury_history` does not grant movement authority. - Agent may propose allocation via restricted port; does not approve, execute, grant permission or change policy. - Authenticated and enabled humans may propose; approval requires another human, with current permission. - Policy/destinations/permissions require human proposal, approval by another human with `treasury_approve` and `treasury_policy`, and explicit activation with `treasury_activate`. Activating configuration does not require the financial permission `treasury_execute`. - Pause requires `treasury_pause`; resuming requires `treasury_activate`. Pausing does not erase proposals, reserves, receipts or initiated submissions. - The client never chooses their actor/role. They are verified on the server before each action; revocation invalidates not-yet-submitted execution. - There is no privilege auto-bootstrap endpoint. Provisioning initial roles is explicit administrative responsibility; the test provisions only disposable accounts. ## HTTP Contracts and states `GET /api/treasury/governance`: cash, observed capital, non-inferred realized result, reserve, obligations, historically allocated capital, current policy, proposals/states and aggregate summary of distributions/retention/buyback of the local model. Detailed auditing remains in the shared history; does not return personal information of beneficiaries. `POST /api/treasury/governance`: typed commands to analyze/propose/approve/reject/execute/pause/resume. Origin, personal development session, CSRF and permission are mandatory; does not accept identity provided by the client. Observe uses GET. Clients/validators are generated by existing OpenAPI. Proposal: `proposed → approved → confirmed`, or `rejected`; inconclusive submission becomes `uncertain`. Execution has a separate native journal (`requested → sending → sent → confirmed/uncertain/failed`), persisted before broadcast. Approving freezes the version and reserves resources. Changing the version requires new authorization; old proposal does not automatically pass under a new rule. Permissioning/destinations take effect only on confirmed activation, never on suggestion or isolated approval. Before submission: reread policy/permission, version, destination, pause, cash and obligations; maintain own reserve outside the sum of other reserves. Serialization by epoch protects against concurrent approvals/pauses/distributions. Reserve and approval are durable before the external effect; native journal persists stage/nonce before broadcast. After failure, consult the same operation/nonce/hash. Same ID with other terms is a conflict. ## Failures and completion criteria Test: journal versus balance; capital classification; proposal by agent; segregated approval; individual/daily limit; concurrent balance/obligation; revocation; policy/destination changed; pause; repetition; uncertain submission and reconciliation without resubmission; receipt/effects/history; resumption over same bank/chain. The first local proof must use empty PostgreSQL with own sequences and separate EVM. Do not modify administrative account or main blockchain. Public proof requires operator, published contracts/asset, verifiable custody/authorization and funding; these dependencies are not established. Local EOA is not an institutional multisig or financial production atomic control. Reorg limits of the journal implementation remain documented; do not announce production certification. ## Input sources and events - Cash comes from the attested blockchain and must match the journal reconstructed from ERC-20 events. - Direct and app-based purchases have receipts/effects checked before classified as capital. Funding explicitly identified in the fixture is test capital. Round model injection is segregated as synthetic; unknown inputs remain unclassified, never as profit. - Obligations, distributions, retention and buyback destination come from existing `ledger`. Synthetic model result/retention are identified as such. Local buyback means allocating USDC to the test wallet; does not prove external purchase or BETTEX burning. - External realized result remains `null` without evidence; allocated capital does not represent profit or valuation of external positions. - `audit` records proposal, validation, approval/rejection, pre-execution validation, activation, pause/resume, failure and confirmation. Existing executor records intention, nonce and transitions before submission, including for system accounts with administrative visibility. - Lost or inconsistent receipt after reorg blocks financial reporting and new allocations, without resubmitting or releasing exposure. Retrying confirmation queries the same transaction again. Quarantine requires proven recovery or intervention, not implicit authorization to redistribute resources. - Pause covers new allocations and exits from this epoch's controlled round; does not undo initiated submission or block login, observation or capital entry.
