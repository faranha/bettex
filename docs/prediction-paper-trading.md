# BTC, ETH, and SOL forecasts — strategy and simulated operation

This specification complements [hierarchy](agent-hierarchy.md), [architecture](architecture.md), and [Treasury controls](treasury-governance.md). It does not constitute investment approval, yield, trading integration, or authorization to move money. The values below are **initial technical limits adjustable only in the simulation code**, not approved economic parameters. Changing limits, criteria, or methods requires a new version of the policy and tests; all agents and BETTEX receive the same version of `risk`.

## Responsibility and versions

- Global policy `prediction-simulation-policy/1`: simulation only, defensive reserve, aggregate limit, correlation, obligations, current data, specific authorization, reconciliation, and pause upon divergence. The budget starts without funding and is not transferred from the native Treasury. There is no public route to fund or authorize; `execution` only accepts mandates explicitly approved as `simulation_only`, with current validity and versions.
- Strategy `event-probability-hold/1`: look for *Yes/No* sides of **prediction events** that reference BTC, ETH, or SOL; buying event shares is not buying BTC, ETH, or SOL. Hypothesis: calibrated probability of the result, with a conservative lower bound greater than the effective price plus costs. Persist method, version, window, evidence, uncertainty, and invalidation context; an Allora price forecast alone never becomes a resolution probability.
- BTC/ETH/SOL specialists: separate searches by `bitcoin`, `ethereum`, `solana` in active Gamma markets with a CLOB book; check identity, result, closure, token, and instrument book. All share the `crypto-correlated` group and the same controls; text search is discovery, not proof of result eligibility.

## Checks before selecting or executing

Only qualify an event with known identification and resolution deadline, recent book (up to 60 s), buy quote for the **entire order size**, sufficient depth, verifiable fees/impact/spread/settlement asset and conversion, calibrated model specific to the result, and an exit plan prior to the order. Record the probability and its lower/upper interval with methodology/versioning. The expected net return is `quantity × probability − capital including costs`; the conservative edge compares the lower bound to the total quote per share. If one of these pieces of evidence is missing, keep the data as absent and **do not propose**. Limit the horizon to 24 h in this first strategy; inferences with a different horizon cannot be reused without a validated method.

The priority compares estimated net edge, uncertainty interval, worst loss, and duration, breaking ties by immutable ID; it does not promise profit. A serialized transaction subtracts defensive reserve and obligations from the *paper* cash; decisions' reserves and position costs affect availability/exposure. Revalidate quote, deadline, costs, maximum loss, limits, and mandate after selection and upon each fill. Keeping free capital is a normal result. Deduplicated proposals do not renew validity, nor do they repeat a decision. The source of evidence remains immutable; the queue has events received → validated → awaiting decision → selected/rejected/expired/forwarded; decision, order, and position are different records.

## Exclusively simulated limits and reasoning

All financial values are **simulation micro-USDC** (1 USDC = 1,000,000 units). The budget starts `unfunded`; once authorized *outside this public flow*, the defensive reserve is **500 simulated USDC** (500,000,000 units), each order up to **250 USDC** (250,000,000 units), BTC/ETH/SOL together up to **250 USDC**, total portfolio up to **500 USDC**; per asset/event also 250 USDC, per strategy/counterparty 500 USDC. The minimum lower net margin is 300 basis points. There are three attempts per order, with 30 s between them; maximum holding period 24 h; exit hysteresis of 200 basis points and quarantine for reentry for 10 min; loss trigger of 1,500 basis points. These numbers are not derived from economic analysis: they were chosen to be small, global, and limited to prevent over-allocation in a simulation until there is a decision from the Treasury. They **do not** enable real operations.

## Maintenance, exit, and resolution

Keep original expectation and entry cost; calculate current expectation separately only with a new validated probability, exit value with depth **for the full quantity**, unrealized result, and effectively realized result. A quote without depth produces only a non-available/conservative value, never distributable net profit. In the absence of a valid source, pause. While hypothesis and risk remain valid, hold until resolution; reduce if the edge disappears beyond the hysteresis; request exit if hypothesis is invalidated, deadline passed, or loss exceeded. The exit generates a trigger, then an order conditioned on liquidity and authorization, and only reduces position after confirmed fill. Illiquidity keeps the balance open; loss limit is a trigger, **not a guaranteed stop**. Pending orders expire, free reserves are released, and repeated cycles cannot reuse fills. Liquidation requires proof of resolution associated with the instrument, without declaring a return before confirmation; divergence between the log and operational records pauses the commands.

## Verified sources and current limitations

On October 5, 2026, the real Gamma `/public-search` + public CLOB books collection observed 4 tokens/instruments per search in BTC, ETH, and SOL. The API presents status and time **per specialist**, questions/IDs, market source, and book hash; this verifies observability, not liquidity by size nor price/probability accuracy. [Allora research](allora-live-chart-research.md) is separate research; there is no verified integration of *resolution* probability per asset. The calibrated probability method, fees and settlement token conversion, executable depth model, and resolution feed/attestation are still missing. The *paper budget* has also not been approved. Therefore, all specialists remain **blocked for proposals/orders**; the public queue contains real observations and explicit gaps. There are isolated internal BTC/ETH/SOL tests with fixtures and partial fill, price change, competition, cancellation, exit without liquidity, restart, and simulated resolution. These tests are not trading history nor economic validation.

`GET /agents/trading` lists filters by asset/state/view with cursor and count; `GET /agents/trading/journey/{id}` reconstructs the sequence of analysis, selection, simulated authorization, attempts, positions, and closure. In the AI Center and the development pilot Treasury, each amount is labeled as **simulated USDC**; no public route executes trades, and production maintains the existing pilot placeholders. Future quote/result adapters must present verifiable evidence before calling simulated execution commands; do not enter assumed values or use the fixtures in a published environment.
