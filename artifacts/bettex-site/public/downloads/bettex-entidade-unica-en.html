BETTEX — entity and rules

Financing: sources and uses of funds

Angel contributions allocate 100% of funds to product, technology and marketing, with no founder salaries or withdrawals. The 15% token allocation does not impose a funding split: agreements identify sources, uses and rights.

The USDC public sale price goes entirely to the treasury; a separate service fee goes to the operating account. Resale proceeds belong to the seller and create no new funding or treasury profit. Capital, revenue and profit are not equivalent.

Burn Lottery and Double 2X

Burn Lottery: one free number per wallet with staked BETTEX; extra entries paid in BETTEX and fully burned, funding neither prizes nor operating revenue. There is no business cap on numbers. Payment is a BETTEX exit: all prior weight resets and all prior unpaid round credits are cancelled; received USDC remains personal.

Double 2X: optional red or black game using USDC earnings already received, without pending credits. One play per rolling 24 hours, 50/50 outcome and no game fee. With 10 USDC played, a win returns 20 in total, a net gain of 10 paid by the treasury; a loss returns zero and sends the 10 to the treasury. A theoretical 100% RTP does not guarantee gains per play.

Lottery rules must identify price, prize, funding, timing and odds before accepting entries. The games are neither investment strategies nor promises of yield; simulated results do not prove execution, public randomness or settlement.

Operational round and demonstration

The operational model has daily UTC rounds. The visual demo uses a separate storyline from 16:00 to 16:00 São Paulo time over 24 real hours, with no real money or operations. It is not a conversion of UTC midnight or a change to contracts. Market tabs do not create independent staking pools.

Company and administration

BETTEX is a technology product that combines an app, a token and AI agents. The user participates in the project's results according to the token rules, without ownership of the company or treasury.

A single private, closely-held commercial company to be incorporated, managed by the founder in a full stack AI model, with no employees. Specialized external services are contracted by need, not internal positions.

The company brings together the token, application, treasury, investments, and program obligations. Final name, corporate structure, and jurisdiction are part of the formalization. Holders own tokens and program rights, not shares, corporate voting, or a redeemable fraction of the treasury.

The mission expands alternatives for economic participation during the transition of AI and robotics. Transparency, understanding, responsibility, and accountability guide the operation, without guaranteed income or capital protection.

Token, funding, and burn

1 billion BETTEX issuance: 65% public sale, 20% Founder and team, 15% investors. Monthly vesting for 36 months since public sale opening, with whole groups 13:4:3. Release does not prove sale or circulation.

100% of the public sale price in USDC goes to the treasury. Separate service fees go to the operating cash. External markets do not automatically inherit the conditions of the official swap.

Eligible result, after taxes and controls, receives 50% to treasury, 25% to staking, and 25% to the exclusive buyback wallet. Without sufficient weights, reserves, or liquidity, 100% remains in the treasury, without future split. Purchase uses administrative authorization; all acquired BETTEX is burned permanently. The exclusive wallet is not the burn mechanism.

Staking and fiscal sequence

BETTEX available in the official wallet participates automatically. Individual weight = sum of quantity × eligible time. Gross payment = staking share × individual weight ÷ sum of eligible weights for the specific UTC round.

Each round pays or does not pay, without accumulation, reopening, or future supplement. Any BETTEX exit, including partial, resets the weight and cancels unpaid rights. Non-executable fractions return to the treasury, without future credit. Received USDC remains personal.

Taxes attributable to the entity are calculated, deducted, and paid according to the applicable obligation before eligible profit and split. Applicable withholdings are resolved before the net credit. Provision is not payment; accounting for and paying the same obligation does not allow double deduction. There is no universal personal exemption.

Only confirmed and reconciled USDC enters the available balance. Uncertain sends do not authorize repetition nor are they recovered resources. Corrections maintain history and unique effects, without automatic debit from the personal wallet.

Governance and integrations

Policies evolve through approved versions. Administration defines policy; 2-of-3 multisig controls custody; executors act within mandates. Sensitive changes wait at least 24h; already mandated routine does not require new signature per operation. AI does not expand its own permissions.

Treasury, operational cash, exclusive buyback wallet, and personal wallets have their own boundaries. Defensive reserve follows the approved policy, without a general minimum fixed at 50%. New operations require independent risk, availability, and reconciliation.

Arbitrum provides the network and contracts. Allora is a candidate for inferences, not execution. Robinhood Crypto Trading API requires an eligible account in the United States and authorization; it does not prove access to stocks. Arbitrum/Gamma public queries were verified; other examples without private access are simulations. No real money operations took place.
