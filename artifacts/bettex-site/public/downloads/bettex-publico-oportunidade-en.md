# BETTEX — Brazilian public, small values and the new economy

**Cut-off date:** October 3, 2026  
**Depth:** In-depth research  
**Sources cited:** 29  
**Object:** Aggregate economic profile and opportunity fundamentals; neither a demand forecast nor an offering authorization.

## Synthesis

There is evidence that a portion of Brazilians seeks bets out of a need for money and confuses them with investment. In the 2026 Raio X, with 2025 data, 39% of bettors mentioned quick cash in case of need and 20% associated bets with investing or saving money. This describes expectations, not the capacity of bets to generate income. [[1]](https://www.anbima.com.br/data/files/BE/05/B0/55/1EABD91008E6DAD9F82BA2A8/Raio-X-do-Investidor-9-edicao.pdf) The qualitative ANBIMA study identifies income supplementation, entertainment, and hope for life changes, but it interviewed only 60 bettors and former bettors: it does not estimate national prevalence. [[2]](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/papel-das-apostas-online-na-vida-financeira-da-populacao-e-tema-de-estudo-inedito-da-anbima.htm) The CNDL/SPC research also found income and consumption compromise within a specific universe of online consumers that cannot be automatically generalized. [[3]](https://site.cndl.org.br/brasil-chega-a-marca-de-50-milhoes-de-apostadores-em-bets-revela-pesquisa-cndlspc-brasil/)

These findings justify investigating a comprehensible financial experience with visible costs and verifiable controls. They do not demonstrate that the population will buy BETTEX, accept a $2.50 fee, or migrate from bets to tokens. The need for income is no evidence of suitability for a high-risk investment. The business hypothesis must be tested without pressure, promises of loss recovery, or exploitation of financial hardships.

The AI thesis fits the justification: technology can alter tasks, productivity, and the distribution of economic gains. However, the ILO points to transformation as a more likely result than total substitution in most occupations; the IMF measures exposure, not observed disappearance of jobs; and a 2026 IPEA synthesis highlights limits to the risk of mass unemployment in the Brazilian short term. [[4]](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2025-05/Research%20brief_GenAI%202025%20Update.pdf) [[5]](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/sdn/2024/english/sdnea2024001.pdf) [[6]](https://www.ipea.gov.br/cts/pt/central-de-conteudo/todas-as-publicacoes/publicacoes/538-ia-e-o-futuro-do-mercado-de-trabalho) BETTEX may present its proposal for participation in AI-managed strategies without stating that those who do not invest will be left without money.

## 1. Who the audience is — and how much they earn

The available data describes distinct audiences. DataSenado conducted 21,808 telephone interviews with people aged 16 or older in June 2024; its segment of bettors refers to the last 30 days. [[7]](https://www12.senado.leg.br/institucional/datasenado/materias/relatorios-de-pesquisa/golpes-digitais-atingem-24-dos-brasileiros-aponta-21a-edicao-da-pesquisa-panorama-politico) ANBIMA/Datafolha interviewed 5,832 people aged 16 or older in November 2025 and asked about behavior throughout 2025. [[1]](https://www.anbima.com.br/data/files/BE/05/B0/55/1EABD91008E6DAD9F82BA2A8/Raio-X-do-Investidor-9-edicao.pdf) Differences between results should not be automatically interpreted as historical change.

The coexistence of an average family income of R$5,402 in one study and a majority of up to two minimum wages in another does not allow one to choose only the most convenient number. They are surveys of different periods and segments, and an average can be inflated by higher incomes. There is no validated average personal income here for the BETTEX audience. The IBGE reference of R$3,367 in average labor income in 2025 is for the population surveyed by the institute, not bettors. [[8]](http://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/46579-rendimento-medio-da-populacao-brasileira-atinge-r-3-367-em-2025)

**Proposed work profile, not measured persona:** a Brazilian adult familiar with apps, interested in understanding forms of economic participation, and attentive to liquid values and availability of money. The research suggests heterogeneous needs: those looking for fun, those seeking to supplement income, and those looking for an urgent financial exit should not receive the same commercial interpretation. The latter need demands care and protection, not a promise of a saving investment. [[2]](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/papel-das-apostas-online-na-vida-financeira-da-populacao-e-tema-de-estudo-inedito-da-anbima.htm) [[3]](https://site.cndl.org.br/brasil-chega-a-marca-de-50-milhoes-de-apostadores-em-bets-revela-pesquisa-cndlspc-brasil/) [[1]](https://www.anbima.com.br/data/files/BE/05/B0/55/1EABD91008E6DAD9F82BA2A8/Raio-X-do-Investidor-9-edicao.pdf)

## 3. Betting as investment: real need, false equivalence

In the Raio X, beyond financial motivations, 32% of bettors related betting to fun. The risk section points to 11% in an indicator of high risk for problematic behavior based on screening questions; it is not an individual clinical diagnosis. The exact format for selecting motivations was not fully confirmed, therefore their percentages should not be added. [[1]](https://www.anbima.com.br/data/files/BE/05/B0/55/1EABD91008E6DAD9F82BA2A8/Raio-X-do-Investidor-9-edicao.pdf)

CNDL/SPC reports 23% of bettors saying there was a compromise of income/well-being and 43% reporting giving up consumption to bet. The published methodology considers adult online consumers, with web collection and weighting; it does not authorize projecting these results for all Brazilians. [[3]](https://site.cndl.org.br/brasil-chega-a-marca-de-50-milhoes-de-apostadores-em-bets-revela-pesquisa-cndlspc-brasil/) The ANBIMA qualitative study reinforces the distinction between bets and investments, without offering a representative estimate of each journey. [[2]](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/papel-das-apostas-online-na-vida-financeira-da-populacao-e-tema-de-estudo-inedito-da-anbima.htm)

For BETTEX, the responsible response is to explain risk, the origin of results, the possibility of losses, and liquidity restrictions. Do not use the phrase “you lose on bets, therefore you will win with AI.” The Ministry of Health describes care in UBS/CAPS and services via Meu SUS Digital for problems related to bets; switching platforms is not a demonstrated treatment. [[13]](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias-ms/2026/julho/campanha-do-ministerio-da-saude-orienta-sobre-riscos-das-apostas-online-e-atendimento-em-saude-mental-no-sus) The Raio X also records financial investment by 36% of the researched population, which does not mean that bettors are automatically investors or that they want tokens. [[14]](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/anbima-lanca-a-nona-edicao-do-raio-x-do-investidor-brasileiro-36-da-populacao-aplica-em-produtos-financeiros.htm)

## 4. $10 and $15 movements change the economy

The amounts and the expectation of frequent withdrawals are the creator's hypotheses. The project's current fee is $2.50 per applicable service, in USDC. The calculations below reproduce this policy; they are not external research data or profitability projections.

| Event | Base Value | Fee | Result before external costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Withdrawal requested, including fee | $10 | $2.50 — 25% of gross | $7.50 net |
| Withdrawal requested, including fee | $15 | $2.50 — 16.7% of gross | $12.50 net |
| Primary purchase | $10 price | $2.50 additional | $12.50 disbursed |
| Primary purchase | $15 price | $2.50 additional | $17.50 disbursed |

In purchase, 25% and 16.7% are proportions of the price, not of the total disbursed. Of the total disbursement, the fees represent 20% and approximately 14.3%, respectively. No example includes exchange spread, network, or on/off-ramp. USDC is not a withdrawal in Reais; the interface's local currency estimate does not eliminate conversion, availability, or costs.

The weight of the fee is material. The person may accept it for convenience, accumulate more balance, or give up. There is no evidence to choose one of these responses as the correct behavior. If 30 withdrawals of $10 occurred in a month, the gross requested would be $300 and the fees $75: an arithmetic example, not a forecast that a small contribution produces this value. Only recognized and available credits can be withdrawn; the token does not give the right to withdraw a fraction of the treasury.

In the service structure, smaller tickets do not automatically reduce revenue per event, but they may reduce adoption and repetition. In primary purchases, they reduce the capital raised per buyer. Consequently, the treasury, capacity to generate profits, acquisition, retention, support, and operational balance must also be recalibrated — not just changing a number in billing. The research does not approve a new fee or performance fee.

## 5. AI, work, and inclusion in the new economy

The ILO estimates some degree of exposure to generative AI in about a quarter of global jobs, with 3.3% in the most exposed category. The methodology evaluates tasks and technical potential: it does not observe that this proportion of workers has lost their jobs. [[4]](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2025-05/Research%20brief_GenAI%202025%20Update.pdf) The IMF estimates almost 40% global exposure to AI using a different, broader, and earlier approach; its numbers cannot be added nor compared directly to the ILO index. [[5]](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/sdn/2024/english/sdnea2024001.pdf)

The regional ILO/World Bank study analyzes bases from 16 countries, including Brazil, with national years from 2014–2022 and points to digital barriers to obtaining productivity gains. These are modeled potentials, not jobs already eliminated in Brazil. [[15]](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-07/WP121_web.pdf) There are differences in ranges between its document and the published summary, which is why we do not adopt a single regional percentage as a headline. The summary is not an independent corroborator: it deals with the same research. [[16]](https://www.ilo.org/publications/buffer-or-bottleneck-employment-exposure-generative-ai-and-digital-divide) The April 2026 IPEA synthesis does not present a new count of unemployment caused by AI and recommends caution with projections of mass unemployment in the short term. [[6]](https://www.ipea.gov.br/cts/pt/central-de-conteudo/todas-as-publicacoes/publicacoes/538-ia-e-o-futuro-do-mercado-de-trabalho)

This supports a thesis of participation and preparation, not of inevitable total lack of employment. The 2025 TIC Domicílios reports use of generative AI by 32% of internet users, with differences by schooling and class, and mobile data continuity problems. [[17]](https://www.cetic.br/media/analises/tic_domicilios_2025_principais_resultados.pdf) The BC's Financial Citizenship report reinforces that inclusion requires conditions of use, education, and protection, not just an accessible app; the recovery of this document was partial. [[18]](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/cidadaniafinanceira/documentos_cidadania/RCF/relatorio_de_cidadania_financeira_2025.pdf)

**Proposed commercial formulation:** “AI changes work. Access to results also needs to change.” BETTEX intends to offer conditional participation in results of AI-managed strategies, with treasury rules. This does not necessarily equate to a portfolio of AI companies, company shares, or ownership of assets. Nor does it replace professional qualification, salary, or social protection. It must be presented as a high-risk investment, not guaranteed protection against automation.

## 6. The R$600 million news and regulatory change

On October 2, CNN reported a statement by Dario Durigan that approximately R$600 million/day sent to betting accounts were “basically annulled” after the recent measure, attributing the information to the Central Bank. [[19]](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/money/macroeconomia/proibicao-das-bets-interrompeu-movimentacao-diaria-de-r-600-milhoes/) Valor also reported the statement, but its consulted evidence is a search excerpt. [[20]](https://valor.globo.com/brasil/noticia/2026/10/02/pix-para-bets-cai-e-ja-migra-para-outros-setores-afirma-durigan.ghtml) No original specific BC table or official transcript that allows for auditing the universe, period, and method was located. Public Pix statistics do not provide a decomposition by betting recipients there. [[21]](https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/estatisticaspix)

The ANJL association contested the interpretation and alleged clandestine migration, according to an InfoMoney/Estadão report. It is a counterpoint from an interested party, not proof of migration or an independent audit. [[22]](https://www.infomoney.com.br/brasil/bets-associacao-rebate-governo-e-diz-que-apostas-migraram-para-mercado-clandestino/) Thus, the figure should not be treated as a net loss, total betted value, or budget available for BETTEX.

MP 1,394 was published on 09/25/2026, with force upon publication according to art. 30 and the official registry. Its text prohibits the exploitation, offering, intermediation, and advertising of fixed-odds bets in the national territory, with reach to foreign operators that offer in the territory, and excludes other legally authorized lottery modalities. Do not summarize as “all betting is prohibited.” [[23]](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2026/mpv/mpv1394.htm) [[24]](https://legislacao.presidencia.gov.br/atos/?tipo=MPV&numero=1394&ano=2026&ato=13fUTTq1EejpWTe89)

The Congress registered the measure in progress in the observed status of October 2; a Provisional Measure (MP) with force of law is not a mere proposal, nor a permanent law already converted. [[25]](https://www.congressonacional.leg.br/materias/medidas-provisorias/-/mpv/175871) The Senate and Agência Gov summaries indicate a schedule, including a deadline for voluntary withdrawals until 10/5, unavailability of sites/apps starting 10/6, and formal closure of authorizations on 10/25. These are dates after the cut-off, not established facts on 10/3. [[26]](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2026/09/28/mp-proibe-bets-e-determina-encerramento-das-operacoes-em-30-dias) [[27]](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202609/presidente-lula-assina-medida-provisoria-que-proibe-as-bets-no-brasil) Rádio Senado confirms the progress as institutional context. [[28]](https://www12.senado.leg.br/radio/1/noticia/2026/09/28/medida-provisoria-que-proibe-bets-no-pais-ja-esta-no-congresso-nacional)

The STF announced questions and requests for suspension on 09/28: this is not news of a preliminary injunction grant. There was no exhaustive verification of all records until the cut-off to exclude any subsequent decision. [[29]](https://noticias.stf.jus.br/postsnoticias/associacoes-questionam-no-stf-medida-provisoria-que-proibe-bets/) Reconfirm status before each business decision.

The consequence for BETTEX is a validation condition, not an automatic legal conclusion: re-evaluate the Brazilian offering and the betting/casino fronts, distinguishing token offering, place of management, and execution. The structure should not be presented as a shortcut to bypass legislation. The “new economy” thesis does not grant operating authorization.

## 7. Profile and opportunity to test

The justification gathers three hypotheses: comprehensible language about money and risk; access to automated strategies and their conditioned results; and an experience suitable for small values, with transparency about costs. They stem from the observed problem but have not yet been validated as demand by BETTEX.

To test, start with adults who can understand and support the risk, without treating financial difficulties as a sales trigger. Investigate expected gross/net value, tolerance for fees, preference for accumulating or withdrawing, understanding of USDC, risks of loss, and token rights. Register the sample and differences between the creator's audience, bettors in general, and those interested in investments.

Before using the projections to decide on a launch, compare $10/$15 purchases and withdrawals with the previous larger tickets; separate collection services, operational revenue from treasury results, and the desired frequency of credits actually generated. Holder and user goals are not demonstrated paying customers. No conversion percentage or yield is recommended in this report.

## Limitations and critical review

Aggregate data does not describe all bettors and does not validate the own audience. There is still a lack of frequency/net value of withdrawals, willingness to pay, net individual spending, conversion, retention, and suitability of the offering. Family income is not individual salary; flows are not losses. 2024 studies and older bases were kept as dated references, not current portraits.

The drafting separates facts, AI exposure models, creator hypotheses, and commercial inferences. It does not use only promotional sources for the core findings. Different studies corroborate heterogeneity and motivations, but not every specific number. Summaries of the same research and institutions repeating the same law are not independent confirmations.

The recent news and legal act were researched again after collection, including with two lines of investigation into gaps: scale/methodology and validity/source of the R$600 million. The DataSenado discrepancies, SPA record details, complete judicial records, and the original methodology of the daily figure remain open. The attempt to recover the qualitative ANBIMA PDF returned 404; it was not used as evidence, maintaining the institutional page about the study.

Fees, split, rights, portfolio of strategies, or approved financial models were not changed. This report does not validate real operation, offering to the Brazilian retail market, AI performance, or income promise.
