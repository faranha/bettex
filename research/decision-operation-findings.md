**Own-account characterization.** A one-company model can in principle be the account-holder/customer and risk its own treasury; the fact that a BVI/SVG/Panama company bets or trades does not by itself make it a casino or exchange operator. The functional line is whether it only places its own company's orders as customer or instead accepts/holds other people's wagers, matches counterparties, supplies game/event-contract facilities or provides betting software/services to others. BETTEX's stated use of public USDC as treasury and its profit-weighted token payouts make the characterization more fact-sensitive than an ordinary company trading its retained operating cash. Those facts do not establish it is an operator, but mean that “proprietary trading” cannot simply be assumed from the corporate account title.

**KYC is not solved by incorporation or an off-ramp.** Betfair's primary checklist expressly requires director/shareholder identification and company/source-use information. IBKR's USDC announcement applies only to eligible broker clients. This is provider due diligence on BETTEX and its controllers; it neither supplies KYC for BETTEX's retail token holders nor proves the app's present no-KYC signup can support all required AML controls. Conversely, BETTEX's existing no-KYC user signup does not itself prevent a company from separately undergoing vendor onboarding. These are distinct layers and must remain distinct.

**Constitutional compliance, current evidence**

* **BVI:** The Registrar's 2 January 2026 beneficial-owner guidance says a natural person with 10% or more shares or voting rights is a beneficial owner; indirect chains must be traced through to natural persons, and board appointment/removal or other actual management control can independently matter. Required BO details are to be filed; the guidance says filing within 30 days of incorporation can avoid the separate notice process. This directly reaches the private company's ultimate natural owners/controllers; it is not anonymous incorporation. It is a filing obligation, not a finding that token holders are shareholders or beneficial owners merely because they hold BETTEX.
* **Panama:** Law 129/2020 is described in a Panama-hosted institutional AML/CFT note as requiring legal representatives to give UBO identification information to a resident agent; access to the beneficial-owner system is restricted to agents and designated officials. Secondary search snippets say private register and a 30-day filing, but primary law was not fetched. Treat UBO/agent disclosure as real, while treating deadline and any exemptions as to be verified. No formation, annual registered-agent, tax, banking or reporting costs were verified.
* **SVG:** SVG FSA search results confirm the Authority administers company registration and has a specific rule set for companies engaging in forex. That forex-specific regime should not be imputed to all own-account equity trading, event positions or betting. Evidence reviewed is insufficient to establish any special operational advantage or current fee/KYC schedule for BETTEX. A provider's displayed country availability is not a company approval.
* **Fees:** No cost estimate is included for any jurisdiction. The only directly evidenced transaction costs in this report are IBKR/Zerohash's stated 0.30% (minimum $1) conversion charge on eligible stablecoin deposits and network fees. Those are not jurisdictional incorporation costs.

**What cannot be said:** No researched source demonstrates BVI/SVG/Panama positively permits all of BETTEX's public token, treasury, profit-linked USDC distributions, international no-KYC retail operation and cross-market strategy without financial/fund approval. This tranche concerns the company as a customer/trader, not a legal reclassification of BETTEX's token, capital raising or payout model. It does not amend that model. It confirms only that practical company-account avenues exist at providers, subject to independent onboarding.

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference

1. **“A company trading its own money is not a gambling operator.”** Operationally credible where it is solely a customer using its own capital. The BVI notice addresses gaming/betting operations and providing facilities; it does not expressly decide the own-account corporate bettor edge case. Confirm the applicable statutory definitions and actual conduct for sports wagers, event contracts, casinos and any app-level functionality. This workstream does not establish SVG/Panama statutory treatment.
2. **“BVI is the best place because Betfair/IBKR will onboard it.”** Not proven. Provider pages establish possible routes and country snippets, not approval of a BVI legal entity. Betfair's EDD is explicit and discretionary in effect; IBKR USDC needs eligible IB LLC status, and stablecoin funding does not demonstrate account opening.
3. **“IBKR makes treasury investment liquid in USDC.”** Incorrect as stated: announcement says USDC receipt converts to USD and its rail is only for eligible IBKR LLC clients. IBKR's July 2026 release result says eligible clients may withdraw USD via conversion to USDC, PYUSD or RLUSD; it is a broker funding function, not BETTEX's public off-ramp.
4. **“Bots are prohibited at Betfair/SBOBET.”** Overbroad. Betfair offers APIs with bet-placement functions and advertises automation; its specific API-vendor licence snippet restricts operating an exchange without prior written permission. SBOBET snippet specifically prohibits technology/bots used to trick the system or wrongfully obtain a bonus, not every bot or surebet. Conversely, neither source proves this particular customer strategy is permitted.
5. **“Polymarket accepts international corporate traders because it has an institutional page/API.”** Unproven. Current international terms and account eligibility did not load in the selected fetch; the page displayed only a US blocking overlay. A geoblock endpoint is a geography control, not a positive entity eligibility test.
6. **“Private company formation makes the ownership private from compliance authorities.”** False as a practical inference. BVI's current official guidance identifies 10% owners/controllers and traces indirect owners to natural persons; Panama sources describe resident-agent disclosure and a non-public register. Secrecy from the public is distinct from disclosure to registered agents, regulators, brokers and betting operators.
7. **“A gaming/operator licence is needed just because the company places a bet.”** Not established. The available BVI official snippet identifies operating/providing facilities; no retrieved authority says a company's isolated own-account wager is itself operating a casino. Do not convert that narrow evidence either into a universal exemption or into an automatic operator-licence requirement.
