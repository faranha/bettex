## Key Facts

- **Country rule / territorial reach.** The relevant customer location is Brazil: CVM Opinions 32 and 33 address internet offers of foreign securities to people resident in Brazil. Under Opinion 32, internet publication generally makes an offer public unless effective preventive measures or special circumstances show otherwise; Opinion 33 assesses whether a foreign offer is aimed at the Brazilian public, including whether precautions are taken to prevent access. Clear country exclusions, effective Brazil access-blocking, no Brazil-targeted content/forms, and related facts matter; Portuguese language can also be considered. BVI/Panama incorporation alone does not exclude Brazilian rules. [PO 32](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/pareceres-orientacao/anexos/pare032.pdf); [PO 33](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/pareceres-orientacao/anexos/pare033.pdf)

- **Classification is unresolved, not determined by “pooled investment” wording.** Law 6.385/76 includes publicly offered investment contracts granting participation, partnership, or remuneration rights where returns derive from the efforts of the entrepreneur or third parties. CVM’s token guidance takes a functional, fact-specific approach; tokenization itself does not establish that an instrument is a security. BETTEX’s 25% share of eligible realized profits and the operating company’s investment/trading efforts raise a material CIC question, despite no shares, votes, proportional treasury ownership, redemption, or compulsory buyback. Those features do not settle classification either way. The project’s economic description does not itself establish that it is a legal investment fund. [Law 6.385/76, Arts. 2(IX), 19](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l6385.htm); [CVM Opinion 40 announcement](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2022/cvm-divulga-parecer-de-orientacao-sobre-criptoativos-e-o-mercado-de-valores-mobiliarios)

- **Retail route and requirements.** If BETTEX is a security and the offer targets Brazilian residents, Resolution 160 requires prior CVM registration or a dispensation under its rules; an offer also requires a registered lead coordinator. An issuer not registered with CVM may make public offers only to professional investors. Retail offerings require a route available to the instrument and issuer, with applicable offer documents and disclosures; for offerings requiring a prospectus, advertising has to follow the offer’s permitted publicity period and rules. Resolution 160’s automatic-registration categories are specific, not a general permission for any internet token sale. [Resolution 160, Arts. 3–5, 9–12, 25–29](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/100/resol160consolid.pdf)

- **No apparent fit with the identified retail dispensations for this single foreign company.** The crowdfunding dispensation under Resolution 88 requires, among other conditions, a Brazilian small-business issuer and an appropriately registered Brazilian platform. The FÁCIL regime under Resolution 232 is for Brazilian smaller public companies and expressly excludes foreign issuers; it took effect March 16, 2026. Neither appears to fit the stated single BVI/Panama company. These are examples of specific routes, not a complete ruling on every possible CVM registration or dispensation. [Resolution 88 consolidated](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/001/resol088consolid.pdf); [Resolution 232 consolidated](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/200/resol232consolid.pdf)

- **Non-retail exception is different.** Resolution 160 has a limited exclusion for certain foreign-listed securities acquired by Brazilian professional investors through offshore accounts, with offshore settlement and restrictions on Brazilian regulated-market trading. This is not a general exemption for a primary, globally marketed, transferable BETTEX token, and it does not clear a retail sale. [Resolution 160, Art. 8(VI)](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/100/resol160consolid.pdf)

- **Influencers / advertising.** Resolution 160 treats communications by persons acting for the issuer or offeror that seek investor interest as potentially part of a public offer; websites and social networks are mass media. Offer publicity is restricted during defined periods, and permitted promotional materials must follow content, risk-warning, identification, and filing requirements. CVM’s 2023 influencer study says influencers are not regulated as a class, while noting that performing regulated advisory, analysis, or investment-agent activities can trigger authorization requirements. The study’s proposed sponsorship-disclosure measures are recommendations, not themselves binding rules. [Resolution 160](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/100/resol160consolid.pdf); [CVM influencer study, April 2023](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/publicacoes/estudos/20230418-air-influenciadores.pdf)

- **Operational triage: not yet cleared; a regulated route is identifiable; legal incompatibility of the exact route is not proven.** Publicly offering the current token to Brazilian residents is not cleared on the facts available: classification is open, global internet marketing may reach Brazil, and no CVM registration/dispensation or Brazil-directed-access controls are known. The rules identify registration/dispensation routes but do not establish that this precise foreign issuer/token is eligible for retail access. This is not a universal country ban. Betting/casino strategies do not, by themselves, transfer an operator’s gambling licence requirement to BETTEX as an investment-token issuer; that separate question is outside this CVM-offer analysis. The unapproved performance-fee proposal is not used in this assessment.

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference

- The specific BETTEX facts—including 25% eligible-profit participation, wallet-time weighting, voluntary enrollment, treasury retention/reserve, USDC sale, and the absence of ownership or redemption—come from the task brief, not independently reviewed offering documents. Confirm them against final token terms, marketing, transfer mechanics, and operation before relying on classification.
- Internet availability alone does not conclusively establish that a foreign offer is directed at Brazilian residents. Actual marketing, Brazilian access controls, language, forms, solicitation, and investor-location practices need review under Opinions 32/33.
- The 2026 CVM tokenization working group was announced as a study and proposal effort, not a new binding token-offering regime. Whether it made later recommendations or whether subsequent measures issued by the cutoff requires a final current-law check. [CVM announcement, July 17, 2026](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/cvm-institui-grupo-de-trabalho-para-desenvolver-estudos-sobre-tokenizacao-de-valores-mobiliarios)
