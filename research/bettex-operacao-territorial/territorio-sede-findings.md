## Key Facts

**BVI — not yet cleared.** BETTEX cannot be treated as exempt merely because it issues its own token or is BVI-incorporated. The BVI official SIBA PDF was discovered but its fetch failed (429); the official online Act page exposed only viewer metadata. Thus I could not verify the operative own-issue exclusion or the current commencement status of Part II, and do not infer that public offers are either prohibited or freely permitted. Evidence: [SIBA PDF](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/securities_and_investment_business_act.pdf) (search snippet only; [saved snippet evidence](research/bettex-operacao-territorial/sources/territorio-sede-snippets.md)); [official Act page](https://laws.gov.vg/Laws/securities-and-investment-business-act) ([fetch evidence](research/bettex-operacao-territorial/sources/territorio-sede-04.md)).

Classification is unresolved. BETTEX pools primary-sale proceeds into an investment treasury and pays a rule-based share of eligible realised profits; the statutory issue is whether that profit claim satisfies the BVI private-investment-fund test, including any requirement tying the entitlement to a *proportionate interest* in net assets. A treasury-value benchmark in the formula is not, without legal analysis, equivalent to a proportionate asset interest. The failed SIBA fetch prevents a sourced answer to that test; the Regulations alone do not resolve it. [SIBA](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/securities_and_investment_business_act.pdf); [PIF Regulations](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/private_investment_funds_regulations.pdf) ([saved evidence](research/bettex-operacao-territorial/sources/territorio-sede-02.md)).

If BETTEX is a BVI private or professional fund, the official Regulations describe a private-investor route limited to 50 investors or private invitations and a professional-investor route with a US$100,000 minimum investment; offering materials must state there is no on-demand redemption right. These are conditional fund categories, not a retail-public-offer permission or a recommendation to switch to professionals. No redemption right therefore does not by itself resolve classification. [BVI Regulations](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/private_investment_funds_regulations.pdf). **Operational triage:** not yet cleared; no legal incompatibility of the exact retail route proven; Part II and classification remain unresolved.

**Panama — not yet cleared; a regulatory route is identifiable if the token/arrangement is a security or investment-company interest.** The SMV’s consolidated working text says an offer or sale to persons *domiciled* in Panama is deemed made in Panama even if made from abroad; offers to persons domiciled abroad are not deemed made in Panama solely because they originate there. It also empowers the SMV to determine when an internet offer is considered directed to Panamanian-domiciled persons. Incorporation location alone therefore does not answer the resident-customer question. [SMV Unified Text, art. 128](https://supervalores.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/UNIFIED_TEXT_DL1-1999-1.pdf) ([saved evidence](research/bettex-operacao-territorial/sources/territorio-sede-03.md)).

The same text requires investment companies managed “in or from” Panama to register unless within the private-company treatment; it identifies factors including a Panamanian administrator/custodian, principal domicile or publicity there, and the domicile of directors needed for resolutions. Mere Panamanian incorporation, secondary domicile, some personnel, or accounting/secretarial services alone are not listed as sufficient. The working text describes private-company conditions as no offer in Panama plus either a limit of 50 beneficial owners/private communications or offers only to qualified investors with a B/.100,000 minimum. Those conditions do not fit an unrestricted retail public offering if BETTEX is within that regime. [SMV Unified Text](https://supervalores.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/UNIFIED_TEXT_DL1-1999-1.pdf).

For a public offer to Panamanian-domiciled retail customers, the identifiable route is to determine first whether the token is a security / investment-company interest and, if so, address applicable registration and SMV requirements—not to rely on offshore issuance or a private-company exception. The present evidence does not establish that BETTEX is legally a security or investment company, nor prove an exact-route incompatibility. [SMV Unified Text](https://supervalores.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/UNIFIED_TEXT_DL1-1999-1.pdf); [SMV investment-company categories](https://supervalores.gob.pa/informacion-de-personas-juridicas-o-entes-regulados/informacion-general-sociedades-de-inversion-registradas-que-ofrecen-sus-cuotas-de-participacion-en-panama/) ([saved evidence](research/bettex-operacao-territorial/sources/territorio-sede-06.md)). “Domiciled” must not silently be equated with physical residence; the territorial and customer-screening facts need confirmation.
