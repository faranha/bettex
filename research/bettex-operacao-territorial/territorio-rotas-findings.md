## Key Facts

- **El Salvador — income-sharing token is a close statutory fit.** The Digital Assets Issuance Law defines an “Income Public Offering” as an offering establishing the acquirer’s right to receive income, profits, or distributions of returns. BETTEX’s rule-based 25% share of eligible realised profits appears to engage that definition if its token is a “digital asset” under the law; no corporate equity, votes, or ownership of treasury assets are necessary elements of that income-offer definition. ([CNAD, Digital Assets Issuance Law, Arts. 2, 22, 24](https://cnad.gob.sv/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LEY-DE-ACTIVOS-DIGITAIS-2023-ENG.pdf))
- **El Salvador — foreign-issuer territorial hook and retail route.** The statute reaches offers in El Salvador and issuers domiciled abroad where they use a locally domiciled exchange/platform or promote/make an offer to potential acquirers in El Salvador. The latter has a narrow own-initiative relationship exception; it is not a sound fit for an issuer publicly marketing to residents. Public offerings require a certified Relevant Information Document submitted to CNAD for authorization; private offerings are excluded. A CNAD-hosted April 2026 income-token public-offer notice shows an operational public-income-offer route, but for a Salvadoran issuer—it does not prove a foreign issuer’s approval or retail purchaser eligibility. ([Law](https://cnad.gob.sv/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LEY-DE-ACTIVOS-DIGITAIS-2023-ENG.pdf); [April 2026 notice](https://cnad.gob.sv/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Public-Offering-Notice_PMARISCO1.pdf))
- **El Salvador — triage: NOT YET CLEARED; regulated route identifiable.** On the stated facts (transferable token, public/international marketing, no known local approvals), do not publicly solicit Salvadoran residents before confirming token coverage and completing any applicable issuer/offer process. The law does not establish a blanket foreign-issuer ban or require a second company merely because the issuer is foreign. Retail-specific eligibility and how CNAD would classify BETTEX should be confirmed. ([CNAD Law](https://cnad.gob.sv/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LEY-DE-ACTIVOS-DIGITAIS-2023-ENG.pdf))
- **UAE mainland/Dubai outside DIFC — do not presume Category 2.** VARA’s 9 April 2026 issuance guidance defines Category 2 residually: not Category 1 and not exempt. Category 2 issuance needs no issuer VARA licence under that route, but *all* placement and distribution must be performed by a VARA-licensed distributor (a Broker-Dealer Services VASP). Its current ARVA definition covers, among other things, rights to receive/share income from a real-world asset (RWA); RWA includes financial instruments qualifying as securities under CMA laws. BETTEX’s profit entitlement and portfolio-linked treasury therefore raise a material Category 1/ARVA question; the guidance does not categorically exclude securities from VARA oversight. Exact classification depends on what the BETTEX entitlement is income from and how it is structured. If Category 1, a VARA issuer licence is required; the Category 2 distributor route is not a fallback. The guidance does not establish a retail-permission conclusion for BETTEX. ([VARA 9 Apr 2026 Guidance](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_516_VER1.pdf); [Category 1 rule](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/rulebook/d-category-1-va-issuance); [Category 2 rule](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/rulebook/e-category-2-va-issuance))
- **UAE mainland — a separate federal capital-markets route may apply.** Federal Decree-Law 33/2025 includes securities, foreign securities and virtual assets for investment within “Financial Products”; it applies to relevant dealings/activities in the State and persons targeting UAE clients, including from abroad. A foreign issuer offering securities within the State outside a free zone must obtain CMA approval; the law requires an offer prospectus subject to statutory/regulatory exemptions. It took effect 1 January 2026. The token’s mixed profit-share/transferability facts require classification; this is not a finding that BETTEX is conclusively a security or fund. ([Law 33/2025](https://www.uaecma.gov.ae/assets/download/86f55732/federal-decree-law-no-33-of-2025-en-regulation-of.aspx); [Law 32/2025](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/4001))
- **No single UAE-wide licence.** Law 33’s federal scope excludes financial free zones; Dubai Law 4/2022 gives VARA coverage across Dubai zones except DIFC; DFSA regulates in/from DIFC; ADGM FSRA materials address ADGM. These are territorially distinct frameworks, not interchangeable “UAE licences.” Also, SCA was succeeded/renamed as CMA under Federal Decree-Law 32/2025 from 1 January 2026. ([CMA Law](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/4001); [Dubai Law 4/2022](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2022/Law%20No.%20(4)%20of%202022%20Regulating%20Virtual%20Assets.html); [DFSA 2025 update](https://www.dfsa.ae/news/dfsa-issues-updated-rules-regulation-crypto-tokens-difc); [ADGM guidance](https://assets.adgm.com/download/assets/guidance-on-regulation-of-digital-securities-activities-in-adgm.pdf/f57b23cc3a0a11ef857b6e984d6ae48f); [CMA transition](https://uaecma.gov.ae/en/new-cma-law))
- **Free-zone retail routes are not substitutes for the proposed global public route.** ADGM’s digital-securities guidance describes public retail participation through an approved prospectus and MTF/RIE route, while exempt offers are limited (e.g. professional clients other than natural persons, fewer than 50 persons, or at least USD100,000 per person). This is guidance dated 24 February 2020, so it is a route indicator—not a verified current approval checklist or authority to substitute a professionals-only offer. DFSA’s October 2021 investment-token notice is DIFC-specific; its 2025 crypto-token changes took effect 12 January 2026 and make the current DFSA framework important to recheck. ([ADGM guidance](https://assets.adgm.com/download/assets/guidance-on-regulation-of-digital-securities-activities-in-adgm.pdf/f57b23cc3a0a11ef857b6e984d6ae48f); [DFSA 2021 notice](https://www.dfsa.ae/news/dfsa-introduces-regulatory-framework-investment-tokens); [DFSA 2025 update](https://www.dfsa.ae/news/dfsa-issues-updated-rules-regulation-crypto-tokens-difc))
- **Operational triage — UAE: NOT YET CLEARED; regulated routes identifiable; no universal country ban proven.** Public marketing/sales to residents must be assessed by their actual location and token classification. VARA’s 2024 marketing rules expressly apply to entities marketing VA activity in or targeting the UAE and require a VARA-licensed VASP or approved marketing on its behalf; assess alongside the 2026 issuance guidance and the federal CMA law. No reviewed source proves a categorical legal incompatibility of BETTEX’s exact single-foreign-company structure across the UAE. ([VARA Marketing Regulations 2024](https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_419_VER1.pdf); [CMA Law 33/2025](https://www.uaecma.gov.ae/assets/download/86f55732/federal-decree-law-no-33-of-2025-en-regulation-of.aspx))
- These are **token-offer/distribution** findings, not a conclusion that every corporate bet requires an operator licence. I have not determined gaming/operator licensing for any of the four treasury strategies.

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference

- Whether BETTEX’s 25%-of-eligible-realised-profits right is an El Salvador income public offering, a UAE security/foreign security, a VARA ARVA, a fund interest, or more than one category. A pooled-investment description alone does not establish legal fund classification. (Sources: CNAD Law; CMA Law 33/2025; VARA 9 Apr 2026 Guidance.)
- Whether a particular campaign “targets” El Salvador/UAE residents, whether residents actually subscribe, and which location’s regime applies. The global holder/user goals do not establish local buyers; actual marketing, enrollment and distribution evidence is needed.
- Current ADGM/DFSA public-offer rules and whether any specific foreign-issuer arrangement can satisfy them without a second entity. ADGM evidence reviewed is dated 2020; DFSA’s 2025 Crypto Token changes do not themselves settle investment-token classification.
- VARA’s 2026 guidance makes securities/RWAs relevant to ARVA analysis but does not expressly say every CMA-law security is excluded from Category 2. BETTEX’s link between the token’s income and its mixed treasury strategies needs a reasoned classification.
