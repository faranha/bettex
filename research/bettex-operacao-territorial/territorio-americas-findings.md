## Key Facts

### United States
- **BETTEX classification is unresolved, but the present profit promise creates substantial investment-contract risk.** A transferable token sold for USDC, with an entitlement to a formula-based share of realized profits from treasury strategies managed by the issuer, is materially different from a token with no profit rights. The SEC’s March 17, 2026 Commission-level interpretation distinguishes a crypto asset from the investment-contract transaction around it; it does not make crypto assets categorically non-securities. This is not a final classification of BETTEX; complete contracts, marketing, purchaser expectations, and issuer conduct matter. [SEC interpretation](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-30-sec-clarifies-application-federal-securities-laws-crypto-assets)
- **Public sale to U.S. residents: not yet cleared.** If the offer is a securities distribution, the issuer needs an applicable registration or offering exemption; no such route or registration is established in the supplied facts. Regulation S requires an offshore transaction, no U.S. directed selling efforts, and applicable category conditions. It is not permission to offer to U.S. residents; purchaser residency and the legal tests for offshore transactions/U.S. persons are not interchangeable. [17 CFR 230.903](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-230/section-230.903). BETTEX’s international public marketing and transferability require particular review of actual offer location, purchaser, resale controls, and compliance-period conditions.
- **Non-retail exemptions are not general retail routes.** Private-offering exemptions and Regulation S are distinct potential routes with their own eligibility/marketing limits; the searched eCFR exemption for a private-fund adviser, for example, requires advice solely to qualifying private funds and less than $150 million in private-fund assets. That is an adviser-registration exemption, not a token-offering exemption and not a retail authorization. [17 CFR 275.203(m)-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.203(m)-1). This is snippet-only evidence; specific issuer/offering exemptions were not fully researched.
- **Fund/manager analysis is separate from token issuance.** The arrangement’s pooled economics do not alone establish whether the issuer is an investment company/private fund or whether its operator is an investment adviser. Those are additional, fact-dependent questions; an issuance exemption would not itself resolve fund or manager status. The available SEC adviser-exemption text confirms a separate adviser regime, but BETTEX’s status under it is unassessed. [eCFR 17 CFR 275.203(m)-1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.203(m)-1).

### Canada
- Canadian securities law is provincial/territorial. CSA guidance says token offerings may involve securities and that application is often determined case by case; it does not establish a blanket prohibition or a blanket foreign-issuer safe harbor. [CSA/AMF crypto-asset regulation (French)](https://www.autorites-valeurs-mobilières.ca/investisseur/les-cryptoactifs/la-reglementation-des-cryptoactifs); [CSA Staff Notice 46-307 (2017 search-result evidence)](https://www.bcsc.bc.ca/-/media/PWS/Resources/Securities_Law/Policies/Policy4/46307-CSA-Staff-Notice-August-24-2017.pdf?dt=20200314055238)
- **Public retail offer to Canadian residents: not yet cleared.** NI 45-106 is a prospectus-exemption instrument; its accredited-investor and offering-memorandum routes are exemptions, not an assumption that a globally marketed token may be sold to all retail residents. The exact eligible routes, jurisdictional availability, resale restrictions, and issuer/dealer obligations need review province by province. The instrument was located, but its operative provisions were not successfully fetched, so no specific Canadian exemption is confirmed here. [OSC, NI 45-106](https://www.osc.ca/en/securities-law/instruments-rules-policies/4/45-106/national-instrument-45-106-prospectus-exemptions); [NI 45-106 search-result evidence](https://www.osc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20090717_45-106_prospectus-sup3.pdf)
- **Investment-fund status remains a separate uncertainty.** CSA Staff Notice 81-336 (2023) concerns public crypto-asset investment funds and guidance within NI 81-102; it expressly does not create new legal requirements. It is not a finding that BETTEX is an investment fund, nor a general approval of token distributions. [CSA Staff Notice 81-336](https://www.asc.ca/-/media/ASC-Documents-part-1/Regulatory-Instruments/2023/07/6104091-CSA-Notice-81-336-Guidance-on-Crypto-Asset-Investment-Funds.pdf)

**Operational triage:** **Not yet cleared** for public offers to residents in either country. A regulated route may be identifiable after country/province and purchaser segmentation, classification, offering, fund/manager, and any separate strategy licensing reviews. The evidence does **not** prove legal incompatibility of the exact route, nor a universal U.S. or Canadian country ban. No foreign incorporation, Reg S label, or accredited-investor route should be treated as retail permission.
