# Regulations

This page offers a comprehensive set of regulations and approved frameworks governing the operation of financial markets in the United Arab Emirates. These regulations are designed to enhance transparency, protect investor rights, and ensure the efficiency and fairness of financial markets through a progressive legislative framework aligned with leading local and international best practices. Users can access laws, regulations, regulatory decisions, circulars, and relevant procedures, providing a reliable reference for all stakeholders in the financial and investment sectors. This page also allows users to review draft regulations currently under consideration, supporting the principle of participatory decision-making.
