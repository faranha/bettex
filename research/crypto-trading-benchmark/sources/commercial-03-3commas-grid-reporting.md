# Statistics Page Overview

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/1722434097/6975cfc1bfb4a76e7aa8f6fbc08a/Screenshot-2B2025-05-15-2Bat-2B2_51_35-E2-80-AFPM.png?expires=1791941400&signature=e132946a5f17471ef2a2ee5b5badcec6c2e52ea5e6b9a04da67e3323b449d6c9&req=dSclFM19mYFWXvMW1HO4zShO5DeA6hjUZlgiGL06RITo1w7tIraCcWauaM17%0AEUTqtXvV1vk%2FTVOzJFg%3D%0A)

Each Grid bot now includes a **dedicated Statistics Page** offering deep insights into performance across different time periods. This feature is available for both Spot and Futures Grid bots. You can access this page by clicking on the Grid bot's name.

You’ll find data organized into three main blocks:

- **Common stats**

- **Performance over time (charts + table)**

- **Asset allocation (uPnL section)**


* * *

# Common Stats (Top Section)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/1525194546/18bd7ddbf52a66873935f6a000b1/Screenshot+2025-05-15+at+2_53_29%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1791941400&signature=0a7f8d870ae3dc9149c50770d6b1aad0c88c2097cb2ece2b8383091cd28e23f1&req=dSUlE8h3mYRbX%2FMW1HO4zdjL6Ge81wUdDcluUz85SwcMq8qjD7RaEs4YU%2BQ1%0As1sL9eP5VAXbKO9RAho%3D%0A)

These values summarize your bot’s general status and historical activity:

- **Total Profit** \- Sum of PnL and uPnL of all trades

- **PnL** \- Profit or loss of completed trades

- **Avg. Daily PnL** \- The total realized profit divided by the number of days the bot has been running

- **uPnL (Unrealized PnL)** \- Difference between Investment and Result amount

- **Avg. Entry Price** \- For Futures bots only; quoted in quote currency

- **Open Orders** \- Total Buy/Sell orders currently placed

- **Runtime** \- Bot’s active duration





  - **Active bot** → Shows “Started at”

  - **Stopped bot** → Shows “Stopped at”


- **Created At** → Shows creation date

# PnL by period (Chart + Table)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/1525234773/91990487aae6152d75dbf933232f/Screenshot+2025-05-15+at+3_24_40%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1791941400&signature=86478e72512ba9322a01b56f750777902213bdbdc31cbb6da819200dec174fff&req=dSUlE8t9mYZYWvMW1HO4zZK3uLjrwMyuu%2BqqmfGHBuk7CgS3ecjLYKva%2Bk4T%0AGOcTgwwSWcCDieNnsj0%3D%0A)

You can review your Grid Bot’s performance over different time periods using interactive bar and line charts. These charts give you a detailed breakdown of:

- **Avg. (daily/weekly/monthly) PnL** – shown in USD or crypto

- **Fees paid** – trading fees incurred over the period

- **Trading volume** – total value of trades

- **Closed orders** – number of completed trades

​

## Timeframe Filters

Choose a timeframe to see how your bot has performed:

​

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/1525258473/e174eadc8348a29bf19ecc4a293c/image.png?expires=1791941400&signature=28d228da3b87a71d170b1528ccc172ac7332ea5ebeadbe6a4ec6b354b6eb3666&req=dSUlE8t7lYVYWvMW1HO4zWPG%2Bni1bkakK52iwMQdCAyaWROiGC8FvrXn1BdA%0AD6o5jTUSsqSxyPCmPkA%3D%0A)

- **1 day** – shows hourly data (up to 24 bars)

- **1 week** – shows daily data (7 bars)

- **1 month** – shows daily data (31 bars)

- **3 months** – shows weekly data (12 bars)

- **Custom** – choose any range up to 3 months


The chart displays up to **31 bars** on the main page and **96 bars** in the expanded (modal) view. Timeframes automatically adjust:

- **1–4 days** → Hourly view

- **4+ days** → Daily view

- **1–3 months** → Weekly view

# Asset Allocation & uPnL Breakdown

This section compares your **initial vs current balance**, so you can see growth or drawdowns in real terms.

**Displayed elements:**

- **Bars** \- Show investment total vs current result (USD, base, or quote)

- **Table** \- Breakdown by:





  - Initial investment (Base + Quote)

  - Current result (Base + Quote)

  - Profit (in Profit currency)


- Profit bar matches your bot’s selected profit currency.



![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/1525294932/4ccb24055d3bbea6d350421a0a36/Screenshot+2025-05-15+at+4_08_25%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1791941400&signature=9cb1fcdbd931831059bebf83b6bbe2e040f93b89d21251ae5e39a14e323001cc&req=dSUlE8t3mYhcW%2FMW1HO4zduuadu4oedq%2FAza2YkU1xU9IQX7A5FLa%2BiC7qB2%0AbFhy%0A)

- On hover: Bars outside focus become semi-transparent and the corresponding line in the table is highlighted.

# Quick Actions

## Active bot

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/2363456188/fae63ff386466e0869a58a5bcd1f/image.png?expires=1791941400&signature=a1acd41ffdb2b7c6f17f3647b2194c954599dea123bcdde52c5959757a273121&req=diMhFc17m4BXUfMW1HO4zfBQuN3me%2FpMSzIsdKWaDs1LssmLOVCmkEuiEJLR%0ARQtebTtuYyP9KEPsQfM%3D%0A)

1. **Edit** (pen icon): Opens the bot settings editing page. **Important**: Changing settings resets profit statistics!

2. **Share** (share icon): Generate a shareable card/link with the bot’s performance results.

3. **Close Bot** (X icon): Stop the bot and choose what to do with the remaining currencies: **sell base currency**, **buy base currency**, or **keep both currencies**.

4. **Add funds**: Opens the page for adding funds. The bot will perform additional rebalancing to evenly distribute new investments across all lines.

5. **Currency Display & Settings** (sliders icon): Open the bot’s settings/configuration page.









![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/1525316309/3be5655548966028071905631e6a/image.png?expires=1791941400&signature=796a2866b9794f60537d93da38e8161a3833a59afc076168642358a244a27443&req=dSUlE8p%2Fm4JfUPMW1HO4zbPHSv%2FS2OQxzHV6pnhMBQBXJ3S8E1nkG0Aa7Aiv%0A%2Fi5u%0A)


You can toggle the display between:

- **System currency (USD)**

- **Crypto asset (e.g., BTC, ETH, etc.)**


![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/2363473715/3ac991f9b6e4e23145dfde2bd70a/image.png?expires=1791941400&signature=5a6bb1562cc0147451a3efd9cc1fc1bb06856037cd2c6254db31843d87a8f9bc&req=diMhFc15noZeXPMW1HO4zYj%2BXikzWEmA%2BCoxPsThX%2BZvG7Ay1hNznZVir5qR%0AoxbMJjklR5BEK5lZQ7E%3D%0A)

## Stopped bot

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/fuovgowa/2082049997/e1db1061bc8acd1a43831afa2f47/image.png?expires=1791941400&signature=ca2c9fbabea91e7b5145ce3bfede3e4526e17447c0c469dee6b73d8b90a52237&req=diAvFMl6lIhWXvMW1HO4zZsWRPNKTwF9BBHdNMk8MTvtb%2F9mMjh%2BaENgfPlE%0ABmrJpwKFN4w3NDNlZ18%3D%0A)

1. **Share** (share icon): Generate a shareable card/link with the bot’s performance results.

2. **Delete** (trash icon): Permanently delete the bot.

3. **Copy Bot**: Create a copy of the bot settings to reuse the strategy.

4. **Currency Display & Settings** (sliders icon): Open the bot’s settings/configuration page.
