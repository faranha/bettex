## Search evidence

### 1. BVI FSC virtual-assets guidance (Tier 1; publication date not shown in search result)
- Title: “Guidance on Regulation of Virtual Assets in the Virgin Islands (BVI)”
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/documents/Sandbox/guidance_on_regulation_of_virtual_assets_in_the_virgin_islands_bvi_final_0.pdf
- Search snippet: Utility tokens providing only ability to purchase goods/services “would not be captured by financial services legislation”; if a virtual asset product/service provides a benefit or right beyond a medium of exchange, it “may be captured under” SIBA. Snippet frames a table of applicable law and whether regulation is required.
- Search: “BVI ICO retail international issuance SIBA VASP outside BVI 2026 securities token public offer”; variants on BVI token issuance / retail public offering / VASP issuance.

### 2. Maples Group (Tier 2; 2026-04-23 article date surfaced in search result)
- Title: “The Rise of Digital Asset Funds and Tokenisation in the Cayman Islands and the British Virgin Islands”
- URL: https://maples.com/knowledge/the-rise-of-digital-asset-funds-and-tokenisation-in-the-cayman-islands-and-the-british-virgin-islands
- Search result also linked the same article as a PDF: https://maples.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/20260423-Article-Cayman-and-BVI-The-Rise-of-Digital-Asset-Funds-and-Tokenisation-in-the-Cayman-Islands-and-the-British-Virgin-Islands.pdf
- Search snippet (BVI portion): primary token issuance, including public token offerings, “is not in itself, a regulated activity”; regulation depends on token characterization and related activities. Exchange, transfer, custody, or investment-related services may fall under VASP Act 2022 or SIBA. This is a legal-practice claim, not an official exemption or the whole legal analysis.

### 4. BVI FSC approved-manager guidance (Tier 1; result dated 2014-02-25)
- Title: “FSC Approved Managers Guidlines - Consolidated”
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/documents/Policies%20and%20Guidelines/approved_investment_managers_guidelines_final.pdf
- Search snippet: SIBA requires persons wishing to carry on investment business in or from the BVI to be licensed, including investment management/advice for mutual funds and other collective investment schemes; the Approved Managers Regulations establish a framework for eligible managers. A specialist/limited regime is not evidence the proposed business qualifies for it.

### 5. CVM (Tier 1; official guidance dated 2022-10-11, published DOU 2022-10-14)
- Title: “Parecer de Orientação CVM 40 — Os CriptoAtivos e o Mercado de Valores Mobiliários”
- URL: https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/legislacao/pareceres-orientacao/pare040.html
- Evidence: `research/sources/bvi-deep-global-02.md` (the HTML landing page supplies PDF/DOC links rather than the body).
- Search result from Chambers’ Global Practice Guides (Tier 2; publication date not shown): https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/comparison/1134/19487/30676-30678-30679-30680-30681-30682-30683 . Snippet says CVM acts when a crypto-asset/token/structure qualifies as a security or involves public offering, intermediation, management, advisory or distribution in capital markets; Opinion 40 consolidates CVM’s understanding; classification follows economic function and rights.

### 6. ESMA/European Commission MiCA Q&A (Tier 1; question posted 2024-01-09, Commission answer dated 2024-06-20)
- Title: “ESMA_QA_2067 — Treatment of staking services in MiCA”
- URL: https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-data/questions-answers/2067
- Evidence: `research/sources/bvi-deep-global-03.md`.
- Search snippet: protocol-native staking is not specifically regulated/prohibited by MiCA; staking services for clients involving custody/private keys require CASP authorization for custody under MiCA Article 75. It does not determine whether profit rights make a token a financial instrument outside MiCA.

### 7. EU securities prospectus regime (Tier 1; date not shown in search result)
- Title: “Prospectus to be published when securities are offered to the public or admitted to trading on a regulated market”
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/prospectus-to-be-published-when-securities-are-offered-to-the-public-or-admitted-to-trading-on-a-regulated-market.html
- Search snippet: the Prospectus Regulation requires a prospectus for a public offer of securities or admission to trading on a regulated market, subject to the regulation’s rules/exemptions. MiCA does not displace securities law for financial instruments.

### 8. ESMA MiCA framework page (Tier 1; date not shown)
- Title: “Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA)”
- URL: https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica
- Search snippet: MiCA covers crypto-assets not already regulated by other financial-services legislation and establishes disclosure/authorization/supervision rules for public offers/trading of covered crypto-assets.

### 9. ESMA 2026 MiCA review news (Tier 1; dated 2026-09-30)
- Title: “ESMA calls for changes to make MiCA clearer, safer and ready for emerging services”
