\[Gazetted 6 October, 2022\]

The Cabinet, acting on the advice of the Financial Services Commission pursuant
to section 249(1) of the BVI Business Companies Act, Revised Edition 2020,
makes the following Order:

## Citation and commencement

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the BVI Business Companies Amendment
of Schedule 1) (No. 2) Order, 2022.

(2) This Order shall come into force on the day the BVI Business
Companies (Amendment) Act, 2022, No. 6 of 2022, comes into operation on the
st
1 January, 2023.
