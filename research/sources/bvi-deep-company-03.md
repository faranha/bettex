# <strong>STATUTORY INSTRUMENT 2025 NO. 62</strong>

# <strong>BVI BUSINESS COMPANIES ACT</strong>

# <strong>(REVISED EDITION 2020)</strong>

# <strong>BVI Business Companies (Amendment of Schedule 1) Order, 2025</strong>

[Gazetted 1<sup>st</sup> July, 2025]

The Cabinet, in exercise of the power conferred by section  249(1) of the  BVI 
Business Companies Act, Revised Edition 2020, and acting on the advice of the 
Financial Services Commission, makes this Order:

## <strong>Citation and commencement</strong>

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the BVI Business Companies (Amendment 
of Schedule 1) Order, 2025.

(2) This Order shall come into force on  the date that the BVI Business 
Companies  and Limited Partnerships (Beneficial Ownership) (Amendment) 
Regulations, 2025 comes into force.

## <strong>Schedule 1 amended</strong>
