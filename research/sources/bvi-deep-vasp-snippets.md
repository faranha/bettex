# BVI VASP research — supplementary search-result evidence

These search-result records supplement the full-source fetches identified in the report. They are retained as leads/cross-checks, not treated as substitutes for legislation or the FSC's full guidance.

## 1. BVI FSC — Industry Circular 43 of 2025
- Title: “Industry Circular 43 of 2025 - The FSC Publishes Virtual Assets And VASP Regulation FAQs”
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg/news/industry-updates/industry-circular-43-2025-fsc-publishes-virtual-assets-and-vasp-regulation
- Date: 21 November 2025 (FSC page date)
- Tier: 1 — official regulator
- Search-result evidence: FSC says it published FAQs about virtual assets and VASP regulation, providing practical information on definitions and relevant activities.

## 2. Maples Group — BVI FSC publishes VASP FAQs
- Title: “BVI FSC publishes VASP FAQs”
- URL: https://maples.com/regulatory-round-up/bvi-fsc-publishes-vasp-faqs
- Date: 27 November 2025
- Tier: 2 — established legal-services firm
- Search-result evidence: describes the FAQs as addressing definitions of virtual assets and VASPs, activities constituting virtual asset services, AML/CFT expectations, supervision and enforcement.

## 3. Harneys — Key guidance on BVI Virtual Assets and VASPs
- Title: “Key guidance on BVI Virtual Assets and VASPs”
- URL: https://www.harneys.com/our-blogs/regulatory/key-guidance-on-bvi-virtual-assets-and-vasps
- Date: 6 January 2026
- Tier: 2 — established BVI/offshore law firm
- Search-result evidence: identifies the November 2025 FSC Circular 43/FAQ as practical perimeter guidance and notes unregistered VASP activity may attract criminal penalties. Verify conclusions against the official Act and FAQ.

## 4. Baker & Partners — British Virgin Islands Cryptoasset Fraud Guide
- Title: “BRITISH VIRGIN ISLANDS — Cryptoasset Fraud Guide”
- URL: https://www.bakerandpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/BVI-Cryptoasset-Fraud-Guide.pdf
- Date: 26 May 2026 (indexed publication date)
- Tier: 2 — specialist legal analysis
- Search-result evidence: reports the FSC's view that a virtual asset conferring additional investment rights (for example profit share, debt or interest, potentially via staking/yield farming/for-profit DAO) may trigger additional investment-regime characterization, and describes BVI AML/CDD obligations for VASPs. This claim particularly requires confirmation against the full FSC virtual-assets guidance and SIBA/fund legislation.

## 5. BVI FSC — Virtual Assets Service Providers' AML Guidelines
- Title: “Virtual Assets Service Providers' AML Guidelines”
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/vasp_aml_guidelines.pdf
- Date: 16 April 2024 (indexed date; verify document date in the original)
- Tier: 1 — official regulator
- Search-result evidence: calls for a risk-based VASP AML/CFT/CPF framework, Travel Rule compliance (collect originator/beneficiary information), investigation of suspicious activity and prompt reporting to the BVI Financial Investigation Agency.

## 6. BVI FSC — Guidance on Application for Registration of a Virtual Assets Service Provider
- Title: “BVI FSC Guidance on Application for Registration of a Virtual Assets Service Provider”
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/guidance_-_application_for_vasp_registration_0_0.pdf
- Date: 24 July 2023 (indexed date)
- Tier: 1 — official regulator
- Search-result evidence: describes registration categories as custody, exchange, and other virtual asset services; search excerpt lists application fees from that guidance. Fees are historic and not relied on here.

## 7. BVI FSC — The Virtual Assets Service Providers Act, 2022 (implementation notice)
- Title: “The Virtual Assets Service Providers Act, 2022”
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg/news/industry-updates/virtual-assets-service-providers-act-2022
- Date: 1 February 2023
- Tier: 1 — official regulator
- Search-result evidence: states that the Act took effect on 1 February 2023 and that it sets VASP activities requiring registration, with non-qualifying activities also outlined.
