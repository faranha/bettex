# Panama online gambling rule — search-result evidence

- **Source URL:** https://www.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Resolucion-11-del-6-de-marzo-de-2020-JUEGOS-DE-SUERTE-Y-AZAR-A-TRAVES-DE-INTERNET.pdf
- **Title:** Resolución 11 del 6 de marzo de 2020 — Juegos de suerte y azar a través de Internet
- **Date:** 2020-03-06; search evidence captured 2026-10-03.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — official MEF-hosted regulatory resolution (search-result evidence only).
- **Evidence:** Search result excerpt says no natural or legal person may advertise or operate games of chance or activities generating online bets in the Republic of Panama without due authorization through a Contract of Administration and Operation issued by the Junta de Control de Juegos. It further states that a foreign-jurisdiction gaming licence does not authorize advertising or operating those games or activities in Panama.
- **Quality:** Search snippet, not full text. An attempted `webFetch` returned HTTP 429; the resolution was not independently read in full. Useful evidence of an express prior-authorization condition for online gaming advertised/operated in Panama, but it does not resolve whether a foreign company placing proprietary bets from abroad is an operator, nor whether later authorization cures earlier operation.
