The registered capital of the company (in cash or in-kind) shall amount to a minimum of 100 dinars (unless a separate law stipulates a higher amount).

This confirms only the ordinary corporate minimum. It does not establish the minimum for a regulated fund or manager, the registration fee, or total setup cost.
