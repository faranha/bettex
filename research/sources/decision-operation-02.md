If you'd like to open a **Corporate Betfair trading account** please fill out the contact form via [https://flutter.com/b2b/exchange-trading-accounts/](https://flutter.com/b2b/exchange-trading-accounts/).

Please also check & note the below from the above page

**Compliance Standards**

Exchange corporate accounts need to satisfy the compliance requirements and standards set by Betfair and the regulatory bodies.

There is a two-phase enhanced due diligence (EDD) process. Access will be granted once these requirements have been satisfied.

- Certificate of Incorporation
- Entity Proof of Address
- Details of intended first deposit & intended account use
- ID & Proof of Address for Directors & Shareholder

Phase 2 will be catered to your company’s specific circumstances and will be informed upon successful completion of Phase 1.
