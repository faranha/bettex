# Onshore jurisdiction research — search-result evidence only

Research date: 3 October 2026. These entries preserve search-result snippets; they are not substitutes for reading the complete source. “Tier 1” means government/regulator/statute; “Tier 2” means legal practitioner or specialist secondary source.

1. **FINMA, “Developments in FinTech”** — https://www.finma.ch/en/documentation/dossier/dossier-fintech/entwicklungen-im-bereich-fintech/  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03; page includes legacy guidance. Tier 1, official regulator. Quality: snippet cross-checked by a full fetch of the page’s own-token passage. Evidence: “The self-issue of asset tokens that qualify as securities does not require FINMA approval, but is governed instead by the prospectus requirements of the Code of Obligations (CO).”

2. **Baer & Karrer, “New Rules on Prospectus and Key Information Document”** — https://www.baerkarrer.ch/userdata/files/publications/1970/FinSA_-_Prospectus_and_KID.pdf  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03 (publication date not shown in result). Tier 2, Swiss law firm. Quality: snippet only. Evidence: offers for securities acquired for consideration exceeding CHF 100,000, securities with a denomination above CHF 100,000, or raising less than CHF 8 million over 12 months are listed as prospectus-exempt; the displayed text does not include the full set of conditions/exceptions.

3. **Swissrights, “Art. 36 FIDLEG (Financial Services Act - 2024)”** — https://www.swissrights.ch/gesetze/Artikel-36-FIDLEG-2024-EN.php  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03; page title indicates 2024 text. Tier 2, legal text reproduction. Quality: snippet only. Evidence: Art. 36 excerpt says no prospectus is required for an offer to fewer than 500 investors and an offer raising less than CHF 8 million over 12 months, among other exemptions. Do not read the investor and value tests as cumulative: the displayed article presents separate exemption grounds.

4. **MAS, “Offers of Securities and Securities-based Derivatives Contracts”** — https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/capital-markets/offers-of-securities-and-securities-based-derivatives-contracts  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official regulator. Quality: snippet and full page fetch. Evidence: exemptions include minimum transaction amount S$200,000; small offers of S$5 million or less in 12 months; private placement to no more than 50 persons in 12 months; and offers to accredited or institutional investors.

5. **Singapore Statutes Online, Securities and Futures Act 2001, ss. 272A–272B result** — https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/SFA2001?ProvIds=pr272A-%2Cpr272B-  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03; live statute portal. Tier 1, statute. Quality: snippet only. Evidence: statutory result labels s. 272A “Small offers” and s. 272B “Private placement”; private placement excerpt states offers are made to no more than 50 persons within any period of 12 months. A separate fetch of a different SFA provisions view was incomplete and did not verify these sections in full.

6. **MAS, “Offers of Collective Investment Schemes”** — https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/capital-markets/offers-of-collective-investment-schemes  
   Date: page result dated 21 October 2024; accessed 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official regulator. Quality: snippet only. Evidence: repeats the S$5 million small-offer and 50-person private-placement routes for CIS, along with accredited/institutional offers.

7. **SFC, “SFC outlines regulation of collective investment schemes”** — https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=13PR84  
   Date: 2013 (as indicated by reference); accessed 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official regulator. Quality: snippet only; dated overview, not a current legal opinion. Evidence: CIS features include an arrangement concerning property, participants lacking day-to-day control over management even if consulted or able to give directions, and the statute’s pooling/management elements. Verify all limbs against current SFO Schedule 1.

8. **SFC, “Statement on Security Token Offerings”** — https://www.sfc.hk/en/News-and-announcements/Policy-statements-and-announcements/Statement-on-Security-Token-Offerings  
   Date: 2019 statement; accessed 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official regulator. Quality: full page fetch, but the fetch notes that the statement was superseded by a 2 November 2023 circular. Evidence: security tokens are likely securities; this is background, not controlling current guidance.

9. **SFC, “Circular on tokenisation of SFC-authorised investment products”** — https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=26EC22  
   Date: result identifies a 2026 circular; accessed 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official regulator. Quality: snippet only. Evidence: prior SFC approval should be obtained before adding a tokenised unit/share class to an SFC-authorised fund’s offering documents and offering it publicly. This concerns authorised products, not an issuer’s own token generally.

10. **Hong Kong e-Legislation, Securities and Futures Ordinance, s. 103** — https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap571!en/s103  
    Date: accessed 2026-10-03; live legislation portal. Tier 1, statute. Quality: snippet only. Evidence: s. 103 is the offence provision for issuing advertisements, invitations or documents relating to investments in specified cases. SFC’s official FAQ search result states an unauthorised CIS public invitation is prohibited unless an exemption applies.
