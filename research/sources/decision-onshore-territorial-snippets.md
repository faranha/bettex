# Onshore territoriality supplement — snippets

Research date: 3 October 2026. Search-result evidence below is supplemental to `decision-onshore-snippets.md`. Tiers: Tier 1 = official regulator/statute; Tier 2 = legal secondary source.

1. **Fedlex, Collective Investment Schemes Ordinance (CISO), Art. 1b** — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2006/859/en  
   Date: accessed/fetched 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official legislation. Quality: selective full fetch saved at `research/sources/decision-onshore-CH-CISA-01.md`; returned analysis was truncated after the relevant provisions. Evidence: Art. 1b defines operating companies by commercial entrepreneurial activity and a main purpose of managing a services, production or trading business; investments must be subordinate/accessory to that purpose.

2. **Swiss Financial Services Act (FinSA), Art. 35** — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2019/758/en  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03. Tier 1, official legislation. Quality: statutory proposition used for this supplement; not separately fetched in this pass. Evidence: Art. 35 frames prospectus obligation around public offer of securities in Switzerland or admission to trading in Switzerland; it is not an issuer-domicile-only trigger.

3. **Hong Kong SFC, Companies Ordinance consultation conclusions** — https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/EN/files/LSD/Legislative-history/laws/sfo/8/Consultation-Conclusion/CO5726_1.pdf?rev=b9a5d7cafd894e18bf584e387d14a08f  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03; legislative-history document. Tier 1, regulator/government consultation record. Quality: snippet only, proposal/history context, not operative provision. Evidence: result discusses a proposal to exclude documents for offers of shares or debentures made outside Hong Kong to persons outside Hong Kong from the prospectus definition. It does not establish a general SFO s. 103 exception for a contractual token.

4. **Hong Kong Securities and Futures Ordinance, s. 103** — https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap571!en/s103  
   Date: accessed 2026-10-03; live legislation portal. Tier 1, statute. Quality: partial search result only; full s.103 exceptions and foreign-only-offer scope were not fetched. Evidence: s.103 concerns advertisements, invitations or documents relating to investments, and materials indicate issuance may be “in Hong Kong or elsewhere”; intended public and specific exception must be checked.
