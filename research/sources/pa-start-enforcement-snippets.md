## Search 1 — broad prior-operation / later-license question

### S1.1 — Online gaming resolution
- **Title:** JUEGOS DE SUERTE Y AZAR A TRAVÉS DE INTERNET
- **URL:** https://www.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Resolucion-11-del-6-de-marzo-de-2020-JUEGOS-DE-SUERTE-Y-AZAR-A-TRAVES-DE-INTERNET.pdf
- **Date:** Resolution 2020-03-06; search captured 2026-10-03.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — official MEF-hosted resolution.
- **Snippet:** “Ninguna persona natural o jurídica, podrá publicitar ni operar juegos de suerte y azar ni actividades que generen apuestas a través de Internet en la República de Panamá, sin que se encuentren debidamente autorizados, a través de un Contrato de Administración y Operación, por la Junta de Control de Juegos.” Search result also says an operator licence from another jurisdiction does not authorize advertising or operating such games in Panama.
- **Quality:** Search-only; the source's `webFetch` received HTTP 429. No conclusion about later licensing or proprietary bettors follows from the snippet alone.

### S1.2 — Remittance-house authorization notice
- **Title:** Aviso al Público — Dirección General de Empresas Financieras / Superintendencia de Bancos

## Search 2 — financial activity, securities and authorization

### S2.1 — Securities Market Law working text
- **Title:** Unified Text of Decree Law No. 1 of 1999 (Securities Market Law)
- **URL:** https://supervalores.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/UNIFIED_TEXT_DL1-1999-1.pdf
- **Date:** Consolidated working text; accessed 2026-10-03.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — regulator-hosted statutory text, with important publication/currentness limitations.
- **Snippet:** The SMV text provides that securities-market activities conducted in or from Panama are within SMV supervision; it lists SMV powers to issue, suspend, revoke, cancel and refuse licences. Article 79 includes a five-year disqualification for specified persons associated with an entity whose required authorization/licence to operate as specified regulated market participant was revoked. Article 52 provides suspension/revocation measures where an applicant submitted materially false/misleading information or failed a licence requirement.
- **Quality:** Full existing working-text extraction at `research/sources/decision-gap-panama-02.md`; the SMV states it is not official and directs readers to Gazette No. 26979-A. Findings in that file note the consolidation's coverage and currentness limitations. Those provisions concern specified securities roles/entities and do not establish a general disqualification for every period of pre-licence activity.

### S2.2 — SMV 2026 resolutions index
- **Title:** Resoluciones 2026 — Superintendencia del Mercado de Valores
- **URL:** https://supervalores.gob.pa/resoluciones/resoluciones-2026
- **Date:** 2026; result accessed 2026-10-03.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — official SMV index.
- **Snippet:** Search result records SMV-21-26 (2026-01-21), authorizing voluntary liquidation and cessation of a stock exchange's licensed operations.
- **Quality:** Search-only, procedural metadata; does not concern BETTEX, a refusal after unauthorized operation, or eligibility for a later licence.

## Search 4 — good repute, prior history, curability and interim permission

### S4.1 — Securities Market Law eligibility / refusal provisions
- **Title:** Unified Text of Decree Law No. 1 of 1999 (Securities Market Law)
- **URL:** https://supervalores.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/UNIFIED_TEXT_DL1-1999-1.pdf
- **Date:** Working text accessed 2026-10-03.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — regulator-hosted working consolidation.
- **Snippet:** The retrieved Article 79 text lists specified disqualifying convictions, bankruptcies and five-year bars related to revocation of particular licences/authorizations; Article 52 addresses false/misleading application material and failure to meet requirements. No retrieved provision states that the fact of operating before authorization, without more, creates an automatic five-year bar across all licences or approvals.
- **Quality:** Statutory consolidation is not the authoritative Gazette and the scope is role- and licence-specific. The negative observation is limited to the provisions captured; it is not proof there is no other relevant statute, rule, agency practice or discretionary character/integrity test.

### S4.2 — Historical gaming territorial rule
- **Title:** DLEA - AS. - Cor (historical Gazette text)
- **URL:** http://gacetas.procuraduria-admon.gob.pa/23484_27822.pdf
- **Date:** Historical; exact publication date not confirmed in search result.
- **Tier:** Tier 1 — official Gazette archive, but historical and search-only.
- **Snippet:** Article 5 states that games of chance and activities generating wagers “que se efectúen en la República de Panamá” must be authorized and regulated.
- **Quality:** Search-only and pre-2026. It does not establish current Law 527's full reach, individual bettor liability, regularization, or later-licence effect.

### S4.3 — No verified sandbox, grace period or general retrospective cure found
- **Sources searched:** Official-search results for the terms/queries recorded above; reported Law 527 materials and SMV working text.
- **Date:** Search conducted 2026-10-03.
- **Tier:** Search gap, not a legal source.
