## VIRGIN ISLANDS

## STATUTORY INSTRUMENT 2023 NO. 34

## SECURITIES AND INVESTMENT BUSINESS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023

## (No. 5 of 2023)

## A NOTICE

**MADE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE UNDER SECTION 1(2) OF THE** **SECURITIES AND INVESTMENT BUSINESS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023** **DECLARING THE DATE ON WHICH THE SAID ACT SHALL COME INTO FORCE**

## [Gazetted 24<sup>th</sup> March, 2023]

**WHEREAS** it is provided by section 1(2) of the Securities and Investment Business (Amendment) Act, 2023 (No. 5 of 2023) that the said Act shall come into force on such date as the Minister may, by Notice published in the *Gazette,* appoint; **NOW THEREFORE,** in exercise of the powers vested in me, I hereby appoint the 22<sup>nd</sup> day of March, 2023 to be the date on which the Securities and Investment Business (Amendment) Act, 2023 shall come into force.

Made by the Minister this 24<sup>th</sup> day of March, 2023.

(Sgd.) Natalio D. Wheatley, Minister of Finance.
