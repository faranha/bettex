**Facts:** SPA’s official year-end release reports 25.2m people bet during 2025; its official H1 2025 release reports 17.7m. The Jan-2026 presentation’s 100,775,427 is marked accumulated and text extraction does not preserve its alignment among CPF/account headings, so it is not a defensible annual/current 100m-person audience count. SPA reports 2025 GGR of R$36.96bn, not total stakes or bettor income. ANBIMA/Datafolha’s 9th edition (5,832 in-person interviews; ages 16+; 2025 fieldwork) reports 17% bet online in 2025; among bettors, 39% sought quick money when needed, 32% cited fun and 20% viewed bets as investment. Reported bettor average is household income (R$5,402/month), not individual income or willingness to pay. DataSenado’s 2024 report’s four prior-30-day wager bands total 20.34m, while its press release says 13%/22.13m; unresolved same-survey discrepancy, not separate audiences.

**Gaps:** No primary support found for 26.4m unique 2025 bettors or official SPA confirmation of 30.4m unique bettors in H1 2026. The latter appears in secondary reporting; it distinguishes active registry CPFs from people who bet. Do not combine SPA, ANBIMA and DataSenado estimates.
