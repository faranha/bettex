## KeyFacts

- **ANBIMA Raio-X 9ª edição (published April 2026; fieldwork 4–21 Nov 2025):** Datafolha conducted 5,832 in-person interviews with Brazilians aged 16+, all income classes, with or without individual income; weighted universe estimate 168.1m (PNAD 2023). The report says 17% placed online bets in 2025; 39% cited “ganhar dinheiro rápido em caso de necessidade,” 32% viewed betting as fun (26% previous edition), and 20% as “investir ou juntar dinheiro.” These are reported perceptions/motives—not returns, earnings, or evidence that betting is an investment. 2026 report excerpt does not preserve exact question wording or establish whether motivation options were single- or multiple-select. [ANBIMA 9th-edition report, pp. 5, 8, 66–67 as rendered; https://www.anbima.com.br/data/files/BE/05/B0/55/1EABD91008E6DAD9F82BA2A8/Raio-X-do-Investidor-9-edicao.pdf; `research/bettex-publico-oportunidade/sources/perfil-motivacoes-04-anbima-raiox-9a-edicao-2026.md`]
- The same report’s bets risk index is derived from six PGSI-based statements with frequency response options. It labels 11% of bettors “problemáticos”/high risk (detailed section: 10% previous edition); this is a survey screening indicator, not a clinical diagnosis. The executive summary’s “stable” description is not fully aligned with the detailed comparison. [Same report, pp. 67–68; same saved path.]
- **ANBIMA’s separate qualitative study, published 02/09/2026:** 60 bettors and former bettors from all Brazilian regions, researched in 2025 via interviews, focus groups, digital/social media analysis, financial-market interviews and workshops. It reports motives including income supplement, entertainment, financial strain and hope of life change. It is exploratory, not representative or prevalence-estimating; anonymized testimony should not be generalized. ANBIMA explicitly says betting and investment have distinct natures. [https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/papel-das-apostas-online-na-vida-financeira-da-populacao-e-tema-de-estudo-inedito-da-anbima.htm; `.../perfil-motivacoes-01-anbima-raiox-2026.md`]
- **DataSenado 2024 baseline:** 21,808 telephone interviews, 5–28 June 2024, probabilistic/stratified by state, Brazilians 16+, 95% confidence; national average margin of error 1.22 pp. 84% said they had not made sports bets in the preceding 30 days; about 12% said they had (the published spend bands total 13% after rounding). It documents spend and demographic profile, not betting motives. “Objective questions” are reported, but the source does not establish whether betting questions allowed multiple responses. Its nationwide debt figures are not linked causally to betting. [https://www.senado.leg.br/institucional/datasenado/relatorio_online/pesquisa_aposta_esportiva/2024/interativo.html; `.../perfil-motivacoes-02-datasenado-2024.md`]
- **CNDL/SPC Brasil, 25/09/2026:** survey fielded 25 May–4 June 2026; 939 initial screening contacts and 800 completed cases, online, adults 18+ who purchased online in prior year, with post-weighting by sex, age, state and income. It reports 23% of bettors saying betting compromised income/well-being and 43% saying they gave up consumption to fund bets. Scope is online consumers/bettors in state capitals—not all Brazilians; web method and respondent base require caution. No reasons-for-betting question or response-choice format is specified in the published methodology. [https://site.cndl.org.br/brasil-chega-a-marca-de-50-milhoes-de-apostadores-em-bets-revela-pesquisa-cndlspc-brasil/; `.../perfil-motivacoes-03-cndl-spc.md`]
- **Health/public services:** Ministry of Health’s 26/07/2026 campaign describes SUS mental-health support, teleconsultation/autotest via Meu SUS Digital and in-person entry via UBS/CAPS. This is service information, not a prevalence survey or proof of intervention effectiveness. [https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias-ms/2026/julho/campanha-do-ministerio-da-saude-orienta-sobre-riscos-das-apostas-online-e-atendimento-em-saude-mental-no-sus; `.../perfil-motivacoes-06-ministerio-saude-campanha.md`]
- **BETTEX implications:** existing research supports that some people frame bets as quick money/income supplement, but does not establish unmet demand for BETTEX, a reliable earnings opportunity, or the proposed $10–15 movement/frequent-withdrawal hypothesis. Do not market token profit rights as equity, NAV/redemption, or investment returns. Make uncertainty, loss risk, token rights, and fees explicit; a $2.50 fee may be substantial relative to small transactions if charged per event. Do not use financial distress as targeting/persuasion. These cited betting studies do not justify an AI-employment claim; ANBIMA separately reports 9% of *investors* use AI assistants for investment information, not that bettors want AI betting or that this creates jobs. Any employment case needs independent labor-market evidence and should avoid fear or job guarantees.

## NotableClaimsRequiringCrossReference

- ANBIMA’s 2026 report shows 20% viewing bets as “investir ou juntar dinheiro,” while its detailed passage says 20% versus 24% two editions earlier and executive summary calls the figure stable; check original graphics/question wording before trend claims. Do not conflate this with 2025 Raio-X survey subgroup percentages.
- ANBIMA’s September 2026 qualitative news page describes 17% as the economically active population; the report’s survey universe is 16+ population. Use report denominator and wording.
- CNDL’s “50 million” estimate and its harm percentages do not share the same explicitly documented base: the stated 800 cases are qualifying online purchasers. Avoid extrapolating to Brazil’s whole population or comparing directly with DataSenado/ANBIMA.
- Search excerpts about SUS cases increasing, “4 million” at-risk bettors and 52% betting after significant losses lack denominators/methods in the excerpt; preserved, caveated in `.../perfil-motivacoes-snippets.md`, not used as established prevalence.
- CNDL respondents’ support for bans/regulation and executives’ calls for stricter policy are opinions, not enacted law or an official government proposal. 2026 ANBIMA/CNDL coverage is dated 2 and 25 September, respectively; cutoff 03/10/2026 applied.

## GapsUnansweredQuestions

- Exact ANBIMA motivation question wording, response universe and single-vs-multiple selection; subgroup base counts and confidence intervals for bettor estimates.
- CNDL bettor-specific base n, recruitment/panel details, response-choice rules, how the 50m estimate was derived, and survey precision under its online design.
- Current nationally representative measure of reasons to bet, net wins/losses, repeat withdrawals, demand for BETTEX, or willingness to pay the US$2.50 fee.
- Evidence that AI sports prediction improves user outcomes or supports job creation; evidence about employment and AI must be sourced independently.
- Fiocruz snippet’s underlying source/method for SUS trend and risk figures; no causal attribution established here.
