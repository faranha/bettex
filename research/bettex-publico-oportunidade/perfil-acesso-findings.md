## KeyFacts

- **Brazil has broad internet access, but not uniformly capable or affordable access.** Cetic.br/NIC.br’s TIC Domicílios 2025 fieldwork (Mar–Aug 2025; 27,177 households and 24,535 individuals, ages 10+) reports that 64 million people had their mobile-data allowance run out at least once in the previous three months; 68% of prepaid-plan users reported this. Among affected users, North/rural respondents were more likely to report being unable to use their usual apps. This is relevant to friction and continuity for mobile financial services, not evidence of willingness to invest or use BETTEX. [Cetic PDF](https://www.cetic.br/media/analises/tic_domicilios_2025_principais_resultados.pdf), full fetched findings at `research/bettex-publico-oportunidade/sources/perfil-acesso-01.md`.
- **Digital skill and AI-use gaps are measurable.** The same Cetic release reports generative-AI use among 32% of internet users, with differences by education (59% higher education vs. 17% elementary), class (52% A vs. 16% DE), and age (55% ages 10–15 vs. 6% ages 60+). Among people with elementary education who did not use certain technologies, 65% cited lack of skill and 63% lack of knowledge. These are adoption/skill indicators—not employment outcomes. They can support a non-coercive rationale for practical AI/digital skills education, while making no claim about jobs created or wage gains. [Cetic PDF](https://www.cetic.br/media/analises/tic_domicilios_2025_principais_resultados.pdf), `perfil-acesso-01.md`.
- **Investment is not universal, and ANBIMA does not provide the needed bets-vs-investing cross-tab in this release.** ANBIMA’s ninth Raio X, conducted with Datafolha, reports 5,832 respondents nationwide, ages 16+, and says 36% of the population currently invests in financial products; 24% made some kind of financial investment in 2025. These are different measures/timeframes. The page says betting’s role in people’s financial lives was to be covered by a separate, then-unpublished study. Thus the release cannot establish how bettors invest, whether bets displace investment, nor demand for a token. Fieldwork dates/method details were not present in the fetched page summary. [ANBIMA release](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/anbima-lanca-a-nona-edicao-do-raio-x-do-investidor-brasileiro-36-da-populacao-aplica-em-produtos-financeiros.htm), full fetched findings at `perfil-acesso-02.md`.
- **Use BCB figures as dated context, not current market sizing.** Its 2025 Financial Citizenship Report cites 84% internet access in 2024 and substantial growth in mobile transactions, alongside digital-fraud and consumer-protection concerns. The report is not direct evidence of BETTEX demand or bet losses, and the fetched summary says its content was truncated. The same report describes inclusion as effective access to formal services, including investments; access alone is not inclusion. [BCB report](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/cidadaniafinanceira/documentos_cidadania/RCF/relatorio_de_cidadania_financeira_2025.pdf), fetched summary at `perfil-acesso-03.md` (truncated retrieval).
- **Betting-loss figures need careful attribution and cannot characterize an individual.** A 2025 Rádio Senado result attributes to a BCB study R$18–21bn monthly betting turnover in 2024; turnover is not net losses. A G1 report dated 6 Aug. 2026 attributes an estimated R$62.5bn net household-budget outflow in 2025 to a state fiscal bulletin. The latter’s definitions/method were not verified here. Senacon has a 2025 technical note for consumer-protection bodies, but the retrieved snippet supplies no substantive findings. [Snippet record and caveats](research/bettex-publico-oportunidade/sources/perfil-acesso-snippets.md).
- **Fee illustration (project assumptions, not a public statistic):** US$2.50 on a US$10 withdrawal leaves US$7.50 (25% of gross); on US$15 leaves US$12.50 (16.7%). On purchases, US$10 + fee = US$12.50; US$15 + fee = US$17.50. This excludes any separate conversion spread, network, or on/off-ramp costs. USDC is not BRL: disclose conversion cost, liquidity/availability, redemption/transfer limits, and consumer protections separately; do not imply a stablecoin balance is a bank deposit or that a profit-rights token is equity, NAV-backed, or redeemable. These are product implications, not claims established by the surveys.

## NotableClaimsRequiringCrossReference

- The ANBIMA figures do not compare betting participants with investors. Do not infer that bets caused lower investing or that a BETTEX audience has the same profile as the national population.
- The reported R$62.5bn and R$18–21bn figures have different meanings, periods, and source quality; neither is an individual expected loss or a validated estimate of BETTEX’s addressable market.
- Cetic’s AI-use gap is not proof of employability, job displacement, or employer demand. Any AI-employment case should be framed as skills access/experimentation, not promised placement, income, or protection from job loss.
- No evidence found for willingness to pay, typical US$10/15 activity, frequency of withdrawal, or the prevalence of those behaviors. The creator’s expectation remains an unmeasured hypothesis.
