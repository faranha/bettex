## Scope and method stated in the report

The report says Datafolha conducted face-to-face fieldwork from 4–21 November 2025, completing 5,832 interviews across Brazil. Its population is people aged 16+ across economic classes, with or without individual income; the stated universe is approximately 168.1 million based on PNAD 2023.

## Betting and financial findings

The 9th edition states:

- 17% of the population reported making online bets in 2025.
- 39% of bettors cited quick money in a moment of need as their motivation.
- 32% said they bet for fun (up from 26% in the previous edition).
- 20% regarded bets as an investment.
- The detailed report reports an average monthly **household** income of R$5,402 for bettors. This is not individual income, income from bets, disposable income, or an estimate of ability or willingness to pay.

These are survey responses, not operator-verified accounts, a count of regulated-market unique people, or a conversion forecast. The 20% “investment” response describes perception; it is not evidence that betting is an investment or that bettors want to buy another financial product.
