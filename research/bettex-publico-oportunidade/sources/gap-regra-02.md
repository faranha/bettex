# Gap research: Durigan’s “R$600 million per day” statement and underlying BCB data

**As-of cutoff:** 2026-10-03.

## Findings

- Reporting dated 2 October attributes to Finance Minister Dario Durigan the statement that a roughly **R$600 million daily flow to betting houses**, based on information obtained by the government in partnership with Banco Central, was “basically annulled” from Saturday 26 September, the day after the MP. The attribution is documented in the already-saved CNN report and independently repeated in Valor Econômico. The defensible phrasing is **“Durigan said …, citing government/BC information, as reported by [outlet]”**, not an independently verified BCB statistic.
- No official Ministry of Finance transcript, video/press briefing text, or original BCB table specifically supporting that R$600m figure was located in the bounded search. BCB's search result for daily downloadable CSVs is a general statistical archive, not a bet-transfer series; other BCB open-data search results concerned unrelated cash-in-circulation or broad transfers. They are not evidence for the claim.
- The available report attributes this flow to bets and says the money flow stopped, but the underlying measurement/method, covered operators and payment rails, interval/baseline, treatment of withdrawals/refunds and distinction between gross transfers and net flow are not published in the located source evidence. Do not present this as proof that all betting ceased or all consumer transfers to illegal sites stopped.
